# HeyTaos and a Taos-first regional partnership strategy

October 19, 2026. This proposal establishes no partnership agreement or content reuse rights.

## Scope and complementary roles

[HeyTaos](https://heytaos.com/) covers dining, lodging, shopping, activities, events and trip planning. Its public business offer includes a [free basic listing](https://heytaos.com/pages/get-found-on-heytaos).

The proposed arts guide is narrower and more selectively curated than HeyTaos. A complementary partnership could combine a trusted artistic perspective and verified buying service with broader destination discovery. There is no need to duplicate every business listing or compete on total coverage.

## A 90-day pilot to propose

**Offer:** a jointly promoted, clearly credited “Discover and Collect Taos” landing page linking to a small edited selection of available works, galleries, and appointment opportunities. HeyTaos supplies distribution through channels it chooses. The arts project maintains verified art information and responds to inquiries.

**Scope:** ten participating artists or galleries, a small number of hospitality referral points, two useful editorial pieces, and two viewing sessions. Confirm scope with the partners before announcing names. Include Arroyo Seco only with local support, feasible travel time and confirmed access.

**Measure:** tagged visits, opted-in inquiries, qualified buyer appointments, quotes, completed purchases, buyer acquisition cost, and artist proceeds. Use source-specific links and order references. Neither impressions nor a QR scan is a sale. Compare referrals with another small channel to see whether the partnership produces additional useful demand.

**Commercial approach:** begin with reciprocal editorial links or a capped test budget. Label paid placements as sponsored. Consider a fixed service fee or a documented referral fee only after the buyer journey and accounting responsibilities are clear. Do not promise exclusivity in the pilot.

**Rights and data:** obtain image and text permissions, assign corrections and set event expiry dates. Request newsletter consent separately and keep buyer lists private. Each party retains its pre-existing IP and customer records. Content feeds require an explicit license, update method, and takedown process. Do not scrape or republish a partner's directory by assumption.

**Pilot decision:** renew if there are at least ten qualified appointments and credible evidence of paid purchases at an acquisition cost supported by contribution margin. The plan proposes these thresholds. Revise after observing the first cohort. Pause paid distribution if the art inventory is stale or inquiries are not answered promptly.

## Regional expansion sequence

1. Prepare and test Taos inventory/service during November 1–January 29. Real sales require authorized readiness. Start smaller and grow toward the sixty-work v1 goal.
2. Test Santa Fe, Albuquerque and Denver buyer programs through willing partners before funding a separate event.
3. Publish a few edited regional itineraries only when the underlying hours, permissions, and experience are reliable.
4. Consider Arizona, Utah, southern Colorado, and southern Texas after repeat transactions and partner retention demonstrate demand.

**Southwest Art** is the current working name. Check existing organizations, domains, trademark issues, cultural implications, and governance before buying a brand system or announcing a launch. The first year's commercial advantage should be **depth of knowledge and dependable service in Taos**.
