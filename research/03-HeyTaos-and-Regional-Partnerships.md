# HeyTaos and a Taos-first regional partnership strategy

October 19, 2026. This is a partnership proposal, not evidence of an agreement or permission to reuse content.

## Correct identity and complementary roles

“TellUs” in the meeting notes means **[HeyTaos](https://heytaos.com/)**, as Dave Paul subsequently clarified. Its broad coverage includes dining, lodging, shopping, activities, events, and trip planning. Its public business offer includes a [free basic listing](https://heytaos.com/pages/get-found-on-heytaos).

Dave Paul's direction is that HeyTaos is broader and less selectively curated than the envisioned arts guide, and is a promising partner. The arts project should add a trusted artistic perspective and verified buying service. HeyTaos can remain a broad discovery platform. There is no need to duplicate every business listing or compete on total coverage.

## A 90-day pilot to propose

**Offer:** a jointly promoted, clearly credited “Discover and Collect Taos” landing page linking to a small edited selection of available works, galleries, and appointment opportunities. HeyTaos supplies distribution through channels it chooses; the arts project maintains verified art information and responds to inquiries.

**Scope:** ten participating artists or galleries, a small number of hospitality referral points, two useful editorial pieces, and two viewing sessions. Confirm scope with the partners before announcing names. Include Arroyo Seco where the experience is real and locally supported, not as an assumed extension of a downtown walking route.

**Measure:** tagged visits, opted-in inquiries, qualified buyer appointments, quotes, completed purchases, buyer acquisition cost, and artist proceeds. Use source-specific links and order references. Neither impressions nor a QR scan is a sale. Compare referrals with another small channel to see whether the partnership produces additional useful demand.

**Commercial approach:** begin with reciprocal editorial links or a capped test budget. A paid placement must be identified as sponsored. Consider a fixed service fee or a documented referral fee only after the buyer journey and accounting responsibilities are clear. Do not promise exclusivity in the pilot.

**Rights and data:** written permission for images and text; a named owner of corrections; expiration dates on events; separate newsletter consent; no automatic transfer of buyer lists. Each party retains its pre-existing IP and customer records. Content feeds require an explicit license, update method, and takedown process. Do not scrape or republish a partner's directory by assumption.

**Pilot decision:** renew if there are at least ten qualified appointments and credible evidence of paid purchases at an acquisition cost supported by contribution margin. These are proposed thresholds; revise after observing the first cohort. Pause paid distribution if the art inventory is stale or inquiries are not answered promptly.

## Regional expansion sequence

1. Prepare and test Taos inventory/service during November 1–January 29; real sales require authorized readiness. Start smaller and grow toward the sixty-work v1 goal.
2. Add Santa Fe/Albuquerque and a Denver buyer-facing program through willing partners, not a second expensive event.
3. Publish a few edited regional itineraries only when the underlying hours, permissions, and experience are reliable.
4. Consider Arizona, Utah, southern Colorado, and southern Texas after repeat transactions and partner retention demonstrate demand.

**Southwest Art** is the current working name. Check existing organizations, domains, trademark issues, cultural implications, and governance before buying a brand system or announcing a launch. The first year's commercial advantage should be **depth of knowledge and dependable service in Taos**.
