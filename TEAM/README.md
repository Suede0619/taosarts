# Shared team

The internal team works across the [Denver Energy Center festival](../Denver/README.md) and [Southwest Arts](../SouthwestArts/README.md). Team membership, person material, meeting records and shared follow-through belong here.

## Folder organization

| Location | Contents |
|---|---|
| Local member folders | One folder per internal team member, with existing reference material and links to the canonical registers. |
| `INDEX.local.md` | Private named directory for the member folders, projects and central records. |
| `operations/` | Private people register, central activity log, meetings, sources and sharing drafts for both projects. |
| [OPERATING-METHOD.md](OPERATING-METHOD.md) | Public anonymous method for maintaining people, meetings, evidence and commitments. |
| [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) | Team-workspace instructions and privacy boundaries. |

Project-specific budgets, research and deliverables stay in their project folders. Denver's researched counterpart dossiers remain in its private evidence archive. The shared people register distinguishes internal members from outside contacts and candidates.

The ART `.gitignore` excludes everything inside TEAM except this README, AGENTS.md and OPERATING-METHOD.md. Named member folders, the local index and all operational records remain private and do not sync through public GitHub. The person folders link to one people register and one activity log. They do not maintain competing task lists.
