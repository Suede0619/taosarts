## Funding and event-readiness gates

Before new commitments, maintain a rolling thirteen-week cash forecast, match restrictions to expenses and protect artist, tax, refund and shipping liabilities. The scenario's art-sales targets and unawarded grants are not available money. Do not add website estimates to the earlier integrated illustration without eliminating overlap and rebuilding the budget.

By **July 15, 2027**, if pursuing a fall event, secure cash for non-cancellable costs, agree cancellation terms and hold an adequate reserve. If capacity/funding is inadequate, reduce scope.

Thirty days before the **actual confirmed opening**, require venue/date authority, responsible safety/access reviews, insurance/seller agreements, verified inventory, rehearsed payments/settlement and contingency communication. This roadmap sets no September event date. Extend closeout beyond October 31 where the actual edition requires it. Do not misdate sales to fit year one.

## Operating rhythm

From kickoff, review demand, catalog gaps, service workload and cash weekly. Reconcile authorized payments and delivery separately. A full event requires a separate dedicated team and approved budget.
