# Taos artists and sixty work candidates

**Research snapshot: October 19, 2026. Proposed cohort.** The project has enrolled no artist or seller, cleared no image rights and authorized no checkout. The list makes no fame-ranking claim.

The selection combines internationally recognized contemporary artists, established Taos painters, material variety and accessible printmaking. Public sources cannot establish an exhaustive worldwide fame ranking or sixty simultaneously current, highest-priced, newest works. Where prices or years are absent, they stay unknown. Curatorial review should assess the cohort for balance, representation and actual relationships. The initial ten define a launch proposal. Later selection may include other Taos artists.

The direct-artist livelihood goal also needs emerging/local artists beyond recognized names. Plan an independently curated second intake once the first service works, with no paid selection advantage. Deceased artists such as Agnes Martin, R.C. Label Gorman and Ken Price material as historical context, estate work or resale. Those transactions cannot pay the deceased artists directly.

## How to use these records

The [sixty-row work CSV](../data/artwork-candidates.csv) contains exact source URLs, medium, year where published, dimensions, asking price where published, observed availability, seller, image-reference URLs, rights/authority state and explicit demo IDs. The [artist cohort CSV](../data/artist-cohort.csv) preserves selection reasons and sourcing limits. **Image URLs identify research sources.** Obtain image licenses and production files separately. Do not copy third-party narratives wholesale. Request approved biographies and write original edited context.

“Published available” records a dated page observation. Confirm stock with the seller. An inquiry link establishes no available work. All sixty rows have `checkout_eligible=false`, `rights_status=not_cleared` and `seller_authority=not_obtained`. Before release each needs a verified seller/artist agreement, current availability/price, image permission, complete dimensions, terms and fulfillment route. Unknown-price work uses an inquiry path. Zero placeholders are never stored as valid prices.

The primary sourcing preference is an artist’s own website and linked social accounts, followed by an identified gallery/seller where the artist does not publish sufficient inventory. Public social pages supplied incomplete access. The research bypassed no login, CAPTCHA or access restriction and claims no complete social-media scrape.

## What remains before the prototype and live catalog

Obtain curatorial approval and each artist/authorized seller’s participation. Ask for their six current priority works and approved object sheets. Request exact asking prices, creation years, dimensions/units, framed size, unique/edition/reproduction status, condition, ownership/representation, location, shipping and returns. Clear image and biography permissions separately from permission to sell. Match originals and print variants to separate canonical records.

If six suitable cleared works are unavailable for an artist, publish fewer and disclose the true launch count or choose an approved replacement. Do not manufacture inventory to fill a grid. The discovery work is complete enough to guide review. **Seller checks must establish sixty current offers and any newest or highest-price ranking.** Public evidence alone does not settle them.
