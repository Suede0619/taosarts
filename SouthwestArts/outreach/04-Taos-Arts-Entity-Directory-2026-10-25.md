# Taos arts entities and possible digital value

**Research date: October 25, 2026.** This is a broad working roster of named galleries, studios, museums, foundations, arts organizations and selected programs in Taos and the surrounding county/region. It expands the earlier twelve-gallery shortlist. It is not a complete census, an operating-business register or a contact list. Smaller studios and informal/community-led groups will require local input.

The [CSV directory](../data/taos-arts-entities-2026-10-25.csv) contains the source, evidence status, persona, decision role, possible value and first question for every row. Rows count named entities, sites and programs. A venue/program row with a parent is not another independent business or potential paying partner.

## Evidence levels

- **A:** a first-party identity or purpose was visible in a direct retrieval or web-reader check. This proves neither current stock nor participation, available funds, access, rights or capacity.
- **B:** named in a local arts-network directory only. Current operation and exact location remain unverified. The candidate domain may be old or wrong.
- **H:** a conflicting or historical lead. Do not treat it as an active recruitment target.
- **S:** supplied business details, distinguished from independent published evidence.

The [Taos Arts Council gallery list](https://taosartscouncil.org/taos-galleries/) and [Taos Gallery Association](https://taosgalleryassoc.com/) provide discovery breadth. Their old or conflicting entries require checking. The Council homepage currently announces renovation. Directory publication dates and page footers do not prove that every entity remains open. Failed retrievals and access blocks are recorded privately, not worked around.

## What matters for the proposed pivot

A gallery already has a public presence and a seller workflow. Test a useful buyer request using its own work links. A museum may value approved context and well-prepared visitors. A foundation may need a carefully scoped public program contribution. A collective may need a fair, low-workload response process. Those are different offers.

The “possible digital value” fields are original hypotheses about roles and operations. They are not researched personal preferences. No age, birthplace or years in Taos predicts a person's interest. Do not classify an experienced local participant as change-resistant.

## Limits and next verification

The roster does not grant permission to scrape stock, reproduce art, use a logo, claim representation or contact people. Gallery-association membership is distinct from joining Southwest Arts. Do not use the combined list for a mass pitch.

Before an authorized conversation, confirm identity, actual place, current operation, role, preferred contact route and why the proposed service might be useful. Before publication or commercial service, obtain factual approval and appropriate rights. Treat access, opening times, event dates and prices as separate live checks.

Priority gaps include informal artist groups, some newer contemporary spaces, changing co-ops, individual Pueblo artist businesses and rural studios. The directory deliberately leaves uncertain leads visible. It does not invent a current institution, contact or mission to make the roster look complete.
