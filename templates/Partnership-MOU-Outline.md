# Partnership pilot memorandum — outline

**Draft template, not an executed agreement.** Complete privately and obtain the appropriate parties' approval and professional review where needed.

1. Parties, legal entities, authorized signers, and purpose.
2. Pilot dates, geography, specific deliverables, owners, and a cash ceiling.
3. What each party contributes; no unconfirmed use of names, venues, patron lists, or staff.
4. Editorial independence, selection criteria, sponsorship labels, and conflict/recusal process.
5. Commercial services, fees, referral basis, seller responsibility, tax, invoicing, and settlement.
6. Artist representation, copyright, image/text licenses, permitted channels, credits, corrections, and takedown.
7. Buyer consent, data ownership, limited sharing, security, retention, and deletion.
8. Attribution definition, unique order IDs, reporting window, aggregate metrics, and no double counting.
9. Brand/IP ownership, pre-existing assets, jointly created material, and use after termination.
10. Insurance, liability, cancellation, refunds, and dispute escalation appropriate to the actual service.
11. Pilot success/stop criteria, review dates, renewal, termination, and unfinished obligations.
12. Signatures and the underlying board/organizational authority where required.

For HeyTaos, specifically define content licensing, update frequency, expired event removal, sponsored placement, source tags, who answers inquiries, and what aggregate results are returned. For a festival/guide agreement, also define charitable versus commercial costs and ownership of the archive and inventory permissions.
