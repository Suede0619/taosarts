## Sources deliberately not treated as authority

An unrelated-domain team page replicates old TLC biographies/titles; other people directories give incomplete or stale employment information. Same-name LinkedIn search results span unrelated industries and cities. Neither a coincidental name nor an image alt label resolves the meeting identities. The current official leadership page and direct user confirmation have priority.

Likewise, Wikipedia, scraper rewrites and short market roundups are not used to settle conflicting tower numbers, legal transfer dates, current construction status or the exact permit/finance state. The register preserves meaningful source disagreements instead of selecting the most convenient number.
