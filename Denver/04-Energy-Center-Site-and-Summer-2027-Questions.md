### Why address precision matters

An older trade article reverses the tower/address pairing relative to later accounts. The owner-linked handbook supports **1675 = Tower One, 1625 = Tower Two**. Use addresses on all future source annotations and request an owner-marked plan. Tower numbers alone are not sufficient. The handbook describes building organization. Management and tenancy may have changed since publication. Confirm current conditions before planning an event. [Older conflicting report](https://www.connectcre.com/stories/discounted-denver-office-tower-candidate-for-massive-makeover/), [handbook](https://www.denverenergycenter.com/s/DEC-Tenant-Handbook.pdf).

## The summer 2027 conflict

The September 14 report places residential work beginning in **Q1 2027** and finishing in **early 2029**. The October 22 panel report anticipates permits by late 2026. Summer 2027 therefore falls inside the reported intended construction period. There is no reviewed written owner schedule offering both towers/concourse during that period. [September construction timing](https://www.bisnow.com/news/denver/multifamily/developer-applies-for-construction-permit-for-downtown-denver-office-to-residential-project), [October timing update](https://milehighcre.com/uli-panel-explores-the-future-of-downtown-beyond-office-conversions/).

The construction schedule could prevent access. The owner must confirm which areas, if any, can host the event. The owner could have a phased, separated or revised situation that public reporting does not describe. The research cannot determine that. A later proposal needs the owner’s current availability and possession plan. It cannot assume a construction delay or access to the whole property.

The office tower is also a functioning commercial asset in the current property narrative. An empty-looking office floor or a small event elsewhere in the portfolio does not establish whole-building vacancy, public occupancy approval, lender consent or uninterrupted elevator/service access. [Owner’s office/residential distinction](https://www.denverenergycenter.com/home), [developer’s June interview](https://www.iheart.com/podcast/759-the-ross-kaminsky-show-20710514/episode/6-11-26-interview-asher-luzzatto-can-he-336567593/).

## City interfaces to verify later

The city maintains separate processes for construction/use, event coordination, public-right-of-way activity, admission-based entertainment and alcohol. Which apply depends on the actual footprint, activities and legal operator. Do not assume that private ownership, a prior concert or a three-day permit example authorizes an entire week, all floors or alcohol sales.

Useful official starting points are the [Office of Special Events application portal](https://specialeventapplication.denvergov.org/), [plan review/permits information](https://www.denvergov.org/Government/Agencies-Departments-Offices/Agencies-Departments-Offices-Directory/Community-Planning-and-Development/Plan-Review-Permits-and-Inspections), [temporary/permanent amusement-facility information](https://www.denvergov.org/content/denvergov/en/denver-business-licensing-center/business-licenses/amusement-facility-permanent-temporary.html), and [DOTI special-event requirements PDF](https://denvergov.org/files/assets/public/v/3/special-events/documents/requirements/doti-special-event-entrance-requirements.pdf). These pages identify possible approval processes. Eligibility, fees and deadlines still need confirmation. Once the venue team defines the scope, confirm the current process with its authorized representative. This work involved no government-account access or submission.
