# Denver personas and decision roles

**October 25, 2026. Role hypotheses for the proposed event.** Use the [current proposal](../Common-Altitude-2027/business/01-Business-Plan.md) and [site evidence](../04-Energy-Center-Site-and-Summer-2027-Questions.md). The roles below do not identify the people remembered from a meeting or establish anyone's authority. A person may occupy several roles.

## Different conversations, different authority

An owner may authorize a feasibility discussion without authorizing assembly use. A creative contact may welcome a deck without controlling the property. A city staff conversation does not represent every agency or establish a permit. A known collector is not a sponsor by default. Respect each person's role and ask who should handle the next matter.

The existing evidence distinguishes Energy Center from the owner's High Fidelity activity and Taos operations. Do not import a different venue's policies, event history or dimensions into this pitch. The proposal must explain its own value in a city that already has substantial art activity.

These are professional roles and operating questions. They are not personality, wealth or willingness-to-participate profiles. The [pitch matrix](02-How-and-When-to-Pitch.md) covers every role.
