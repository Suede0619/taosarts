## Findings that change the pitch

1. **Rentals are the current published strategy.** A June 2026 developer interview describes rental housing, with flexibility to change strategy. The host proposition should emphasize future leasing interest, retail/office prospects and public confidence. It should not promise condominium buyers. [Developer strategy evidence](01-Developer-Rentals-and-Existing-Events.md)
2. **There is an existing creative operation.** Advertised music/performance activity at High Fidelity Plaza supports the cultural-activation premise. It does not establish public-event approval or vacancy at Energy Center. Its reported residential construction period overlaps summer 2027. [Venue evidence](01-Developer-Rentals-and-Existing-Events.md)
3. **Do not offer an undefined share of “proceeds.”** In the model $3m artwork-sales case, 10–20% of art GMV is $300,000–$600,000. The same percentages applied to a $160,000 operating surplus are $16,000–$32,000 before tax and reserves. Those are different transactions. [Economics](02-Venue-Deal-and-Sales-Economics.md)
4. **Pay for essential work. Add upside.** Founders may deliberately accept bounded entrepreneurial risk. Professional employees, contractors, artists and production studios need appropriate funded terms. A share of uncertain profits is not a substitute for a production budget. [Compensation](05-Team-Compensation-and-Seed-Funding.md)
5. **The most urgent recruit is an experienced executive producer, alongside a collector-sales lead.** A famous installation artist does not replace either. The candidate research includes local operators, the owner's creative collaborators, art-market advisers, technical suppliers and international installation studios. The project has contacted none of them. [Team research](04-Team-Roles-and-Candidate-Research.md)
6. **Potential public funding is narrower than the opportunity sounds.** Current DDA arts criteria emphasize outdoor space/reusable infrastructure. DENVER CREATES' 2026 round has closed. P.S. The city has paused You Are Here. Bonfils-Stanton lists events as generally ineligible. These are not committed seed sources. [Funding assessment](05-Team-Compensation-and-Seed-Funding.md)
