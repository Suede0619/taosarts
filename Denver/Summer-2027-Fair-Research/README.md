# Denver summer 2027 art fair — feasibility research

**Research date: October 24, 2026. Status: research and decision support, not an approved event, business plan, pitch deck or offer.** November 1, 2026 remains project day one. No event dates, duration, footprint, funding, team appointments or commercial terms are agreed.

The strongest opportunity is an art market with an extraordinary public exhibition, hosted in a precisely defined part of a redevelopment project. Its value would come from completed art purchases, a credible collector experience, paid creative work and measurable benefits for the host. Filling two towers is an ambition requiring substantially more evidence than an attractive concept.

## Findings that change the pitch

1. **Rentals are the current published strategy.** A June 2026 developer interview describes rental housing, with flexibility to change strategy. The host proposition should emphasize future leasing interest, retail/office prospects and public confidence; it should not promise condominium buyers. [Developer strategy evidence](01-Developer-Rentals-and-Existing-Events.md)
2. **There is an existing creative operation.** Advertised music/performance activity at High Fidelity Plaza supports the cultural-activation premise. It does not establish public-event approval or vacancy at Energy Center. Its reported residential construction period overlaps summer 2027. [Venue evidence](01-Developer-Rentals-and-Existing-Events.md)
3. **Do not offer an undefined share of “proceeds.”** In the illustrative $3m artwork-sales case, 10–20% of art GMV is $300,000–$600,000. The same percentages applied to a $160,000 operating surplus are $16,000–$32,000 before tax and reserves. Those are different transactions. [Economics](02-Venue-Deal-and-Sales-Economics.md)
4. **Pay for essential work; add upside.** Founders may deliberately accept bounded entrepreneurial risk. Professional employees, contractors, artists and production studios need appropriate funded terms. A share of uncertain profits is not a substitute for a production budget. [Compensation](05-Team-Compensation-and-Seed-Funding.md)
5. **The most urgent recruit is an experienced executive producer, alongside a collector-sales lead.** A famous installation artist does not replace either. The candidate research includes local operators, the owner's existing creative ecosystem, art-market advisers, technical suppliers and international installation studios. None has been contacted. [Team research](04-Team-Roles-and-Candidate-Research.md)
6. **Potential public funding is narrower than the opportunity sounds.** Current DDA arts criteria emphasize outdoor space/reusable infrastructure; DENVER CREATES' 2026 round has closed; P.S. You Are Here is paused; Bonfils-Stanton lists events as generally ineligible. These are not committed seed sources. [Funding assessment](05-Team-Compensation-and-Seed-Funding.md)
