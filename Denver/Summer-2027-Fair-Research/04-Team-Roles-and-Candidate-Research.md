## Founding responsibilities

The public role design is anonymous. A local ignored working note maps it to the three existing participants. No person has accepted an appointment, ownership allocation or pay agreement.

| Proposed founding role | Accountable work | Necessary boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Founding artistic director / gallery relationships | Curatorial thesis, artist/gallerist relationships, selection process, collector conversations and cultural credibility | A gallery or network background alone does not establish capacity to produce a public event across an office complex |
| Founding platform/product and program lead | Digital experience, CRM requirements, production coordination, decision register, measurement and accountable work tracking | Must not become the sole event producer, CFO, safety approver and legal decision maker |
| Brand strategy and design lead | Naming/positioning process, identity, campaign, art direction, digital/physical design system, wayfinding and production standards | Commission a paid, capacity-matched scope. Extensive installation engineering and all website implementation are separate responsibilities |

Give each a defined scope, authority, capacity and pay. A title is not evidence of available hours. Any equal ownership, profit split or salary decision requires separate agreement. This research supplies none.

Several functions can initially be fractional or combined where competence and conflicts allow. Do not combine finance control, seller money handling and unchecked artistic spending under one person. Safety stop authority must remain effective even when founders or sponsors want the show to proceed.

## Who to consider first

The best first conversation would be a **paid producer/code feasibility scope**, accompanied by an owner-approved discussion with the existing Human Resources/Atelier creative operation. A collector-market adviser should test whether the proposed work and buyer proposition justify travel. Only then commission a large signature work.

Consider Two Parts for production discovery, Jana Novak or CherryArts for fair-operations advice, and Robischon, K Contemporary, NINE dot ARTS or an independent adviser for market research. Jensen Hughes could assess code feasibility. UOVO could scope artwork operations. All roles require agreement. These candidates offer alternatives for different functions. The plan does not propose appointing all of them. Existing festivals and galleries have their own interests: seek fair compensation and disclose competition/conflicts.

Android Jones and Threyda are relevant to an art/music experience. Threyda's ARCHETYPE program combines gallery activity, live visuals and a major music venue. That is useful evidence of a creative network. Neither an impressive visual performance nor a following establishes high-value collector purchases. Ask which work can sell, to which buyers and under which representation terms. Fund production separately from performer recruitment.

## Candidate selection after recruitment authority

Score candidates on relevant delivered work, actual availability, appropriate fee/cash terms, collector conversion or operational outcomes relevant to their job, local trust, safety/financial discipline, references, collaboration and documented conflicts. A broad numerical score is not assigned here because interviews, fees and reference checks are missing.

For the producer, obtain at least two comparable-event references, including venue/property or permitting counterparts where possible. Ask about the actual role, footprint, budget responsibility, staffing and incidents on each comparable event. For collector development, validate relevant market experience without asking anyone to disclose client identities or transfer a private list. For an artist/studio, request licensing, technical rider, setup/strike, travel, insurance and production scope before comparing a fee.

A later recruiting brief should specify the paid discovery deliverable, approximate required availability, budget authority, decision rights, conflicts, contracting entity, IP/data responsibilities and no-launch/cancellation terms. Define the scope, funding and responsibilities before offering a percentage.

## Fairness and independence

Pay Denver and Southwest creative participants for actual work. Include emerging practitioners without presenting established institutions as endorsers. Keep underwriting and gallery selection separate. A gallery adviser should recuse from decisions favoring represented artists. A corporate art consultant's client interests require disclosure. Institutional staff need permission for outside work.

The proposed team can be ambitious and international while the event remains locally accountable. The research does not identify a single person who can legitimately supply every competency, an entire collector pipeline and unpaid labor.
