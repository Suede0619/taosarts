# Evidence, source register and unresolved decisions

**Research cut-off: October 24, 2026.** Source metadata is also available as [JSON](data/sources.json). The prior [Denver discovery source register](../05-News-and-Source-Register.md) remains the detailed acquisition/leadership record.

## Method and limits

The review combined first-party developer/studio/venue material, publicly accessible event listings, city/funder/labor guidance, professional portfolios, selected reporting and preliminary naming searches. **68 indexed source URLs were requested for local retention: 65 HTTP 200, two HTTP 403 and one fetch failure.** Web-reader extracts supplement source S16 (official development-site copy), S48 and S65. Retained HTML/PDF bodies, their SHA256 manifest and the separate web-reader captures are ignored under local `private/sources/`.

An HTTP 200 is a retrieval result, not proof of unrestricted full text, current facts, a completed event or a contract. Some pages have scripts, paywalls, old FAQs, future-year headings or stale “apply/upcoming” labels. The archive is not a complete mirror or a transcription of audio/video. The developer interview has a public transcript; its rental discussion was checked directly. No ownership/title search, issued-permit file, nonpublic financial record, attendee count, seller ledger, candidate reference or owner construction schedule was obtained.

The thirty [candidate records](data/candidate-longlist.csv) are source-based suitability hypotheses. Current professional roles were checked against the linked pages; no capacity, quote, willingness, reference or conflict clearance is established. All money inputs in the [standalone model](data/model-inputs.json) are **original unvalidated assumptions**, not research findings about achievable sales or actual costs.

## Evidence hierarchy

| Evidence class | How it is used |
|---|---|
| Published first-party facts | State who publishes the claim, applicable date and the boundary of what it proves |
| Secondary reporting | Preserve publication date and discrepancies; a reported application/schedule is not an issued permit |
| Supplied project context | Defines questions/ambition; not independently verified agreement or unanimous minutes; named account remains private |
| Analytical proposal | Label explicitly; includes roles, fees, case inputs, names and possible deal structures |
| Unknown | Remain unknown; do not fill with a plausible number, identify a person by spelling similarity or infer a partner commitment |
