## Method and limits

The review combined first-party developer/studio/venue material, publicly accessible event listings, city/funder/labor guidance, professional portfolios, selected reporting and preliminary naming searches. **Local retrieval requested 68 indexed source URLs: 65 HTTP 200, two HTTP 403 and one fetch failure.** Web-reader extracts supplement S16, S48 and S65. Local Git rules ignore retained bodies, the SHA256 manifest and web-reader captures under `private/sources/`.

HTTP 200 confirms a server response. It establishes neither complete access nor current facts, event completion or a contract. Some pages have scripts, paywalls, old FAQs, future-year headings or stale “apply/upcoming” labels. The archive is not a complete mirror or a transcription of audio/video. The developer interview has a public transcript. The review checked the rental discussion directly. The review contains no title search, issued-permit file, private financial record, attendee count, seller ledger, candidate reference or owner construction schedule.

The thirty [candidate records](data/candidate-longlist.csv) are source-based suitability hypotheses. The linked pages describe current professional roles. Capacity, quotes, willingness, references and conflict clearance remain unknown. The [standalone model](data/model-inputs.json) uses **original assumptions without demand or cost validation**.

## Evidence hierarchy

| Evidence class | Use in this research |
|---|---|
| Published first-party facts | State who publishes the claim, applicable date and the boundary of what it proves |
| Secondary reporting | Preserve publication date and discrepancies. A reported application/schedule is not an issued permit |
| Supplied project context | Defines questions/ambition. Not independently verified agreement or unanimous minutes. Named account remains private |
| Analytical proposal | Label explicitly. Includes roles, fees, case inputs, names and possible deal structures |
| Unknown | Remain unknown. Do not fill with a plausible number, identify a person by spelling similarity or infer a partner commitment |
