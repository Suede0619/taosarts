## Principal findings

1. **Atelier 111 is more than a gallery.** Its own site identifies a multidisciplinary design studio and The Luzzatto Company’s in-house creative practice. Its public Taos venue combines exhibitions, a workshop, retail art and performance. The corporate site identifies Atelier as a tenant/design headquarters at 101 Camino de la Placita. A separate legal ownership chart was not obtained. [Atelier about](https://www.atelier111.org/about), [property record published by the company](https://www.luzzatto.company/retail/101-camino-de-la-placita).
2. **The Denver acquisitions cover four towers in two complexes:** 621/633 17th Street, now High Fidelity Plaza, and 1625/1675 Broadway, Denver Energy Center. Reported purchase prices are $3.2m and $5.25m–$5.3m, reflecting different reporting precision, respectively. These low acquisition prices are distinct from conversion budgets in the hundreds of millions. [Acquisition reporting](https://old.businessden.com/2025/04/04/downtown-towers-sell-for-3m-700-unit-residential-conversion-planned/), [Energy Center reporting](https://businessden.com/luzzatto-asks-ddda-for-29m-for-second-residential-conversion/).
3. **Summer 2027 has a material timing conflict.** September reporting gives Energy Center residential construction from Q1 2027 through early 2029. An October 22 report still describes permits as expected by late 2026. Neither establishes that both towers and a concourse are available for an art event. [Construction report](https://www.bisnow.com/news/denver/multifamily/developer-applies-for-construction-permit-for-downtown-denver-office-to-residential-project), [October 22 update](https://milehighcre.com/uli-panel-explores-the-future-of-downtown-beyond-office-conversions/).
4. **The owner already presents cultural events at Denver’s High Fidelity complex.** The proposal must explain what it adds. Large office-building art takeovers also have international precedents. No reviewed source establishes a worldwide first, projected attendance, collector conversion or art-sales result for a new event. [Existing Denver programming](https://therooster.com/articles/to-gladden-the-heart-denver-event/), [Artomatic building activation](https://goldentriangledc.com/2024/03/04/vacant-office-building-in-dcs-golden-triangle-activates-with-artomatic-event-ahead-of-residential-conversion/).
