## Selection and engagement

For each key lead, verify comparable role/scale, actual budget responsibility, references, availability, all-in fee, conflicts, professional coverage, scope, authority and cancellation terms. Gallery/artist contacts must remain consensual. The plan assumes no transfer of private client lists.

A role brief states the result, deliverables, dates, accepted hours, authority and dependencies. It defines fees, payment dates, expenses, rights, data access, professional duties and success criteria. Include exit terms and backup coverage. Confirm capacity before treating the role as staffed.

## Workload and show coverage

Do not assign full-time obligations to a fractional participant. The 365-day calendar assigns functions. It does not prove that one person can perform every action. Each weekly review resolves accepted capacity, priorities and deferrals.

Guided-service planning uses staggered departures and proposed 15-seat groups. Test actual routes, staffing, backup, breaks and approved capacity. Workshops need separate paid instructors for simultaneous rooms. Gallery/seller, registrar, help, security and medical coverage continue during staff breaks. Night programs require separate approved coverage and recovery time.

Appropriate labor/tax advice determines employee/contractor treatment. Profit participation cannot replace required pay. Founder equity/vesting and event bonuses are separate agreements.

## Operating cadence and authority

Review evidence, cash and task dependencies weekly. Use short working sessions within each function and daily briefings during build and show. Keep accepted roles and decisions in private operations. Use one accountable owner and backup per deliverable. No sponsor or founder can overrule qualified safety stop authority.

Hire only when usable cash covers the scope and liabilities. Check timing as well as annual surplus. If seed/facility/funding fails, reduce scope or stop new commitments.
