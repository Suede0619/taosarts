## Selection and engagement

For each key lead, verify comparable role/scale, actual budget responsibility, references, availability, all-in fee, conflicts, professional coverage, scope, authority and cancellation terms. Gallery/artist contacts must remain consensual; no transfer of private client lists is assumed.

Create a short role brief: result required; deliverables; start/end; accepted hours; decision authority; dependencies; fee/pay schedule; expenses; rights/data; professional responsibilities; success criteria; exit and backup. A title without capacity is not staffing.

## Workload and show coverage

Do not assign full-time obligations to a fractional participant. The 365-day calendar assigns functions; it does not prove that one person can perform every action. Each weekly review resolves accepted capacity, priorities and deferrals.

Guided-service planning uses staggered departures and proposed 15-seat groups; actual route, staff/backup, breaks and approved capacity must be tested. Workshops need separate paid instructors for simultaneous rooms. Gallery/seller, registrar, help, security and medical coverage continue during staff breaks. Night programs require separate approved coverage and recovery time.

Appropriate labor/tax advice determines employee/contractor treatment. Profit participation cannot replace required pay. Founder equity/vesting and event bonuses are separate agreements.

## Operating cadence and authority

Weekly evidence/cash/critical-path review; small function working sessions; daily build/show briefing; private accepted-role and decision records. Use one accountable owner and backup per deliverable. No sponsor or founder can overrule qualified safety stop authority.

Hiring is conditional on funded usable cash, scope and liabilities, not only an optimistic annual surplus. If seed/facility/funding fails, reduce scope or stop new commitments.
