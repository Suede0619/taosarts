# Risk, crisis and contingency plan

**Working risk register.** Rate/probability requires actual site, quotes and professional review. This identifies triggers and decisions. It does not certify safe operation.

| Risk | Leading indicator / trigger | Responsible function | Response / contingency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Construction/possession conflict | No signed usable window or changed contractor plan | Producer / owner | G1/G5 rescope, approved alternate site or cancel before exposure |
| Unapproved use/capacity | Missing required review/inspection or unsafe zone | Producer / code specialists | Do not open affected space. Revise access/program/refunds |
| Accessibility failure | Essential route/service unavailable | Access / producer | Correct professionally. Provide appropriate alternatives. No false accessible claim |
| Sponsor shortfall | Contracted/collected eligible cash below gate | Commercial / finance | Freeze spend. Reduce scope or no-launch. No seller-money bridge |
| Cash/refund gap | Available cash after holds negative without committed facility | Finance | Secure lawful financing/usable cash or stop commitments |
| Cost overrun | Quote/change exceeds funded envelope | Producer / finance | Change control. Use actual contingency only by authority. Remove scope |
| Unproven ticket value | Weak paid conversion or guide-capacity test | Product / marketing | Reprice/reduce income assumptions and find real funding before launch |
| Gallery optional-service uptake | Fewer buyers tha modeled | Artistic / commercial / finance | Revise revenue. Preserve independent free selection |
| Weak collector demand | Unqualified list or few useful appointments | Collector / artistic | Improve work/service/fit. Resize targets and scope |
| Unavailable star/artist | Nonresponse, declined scope or travel failure | Artistic / producer | Core works without a celebrity. Alternative with artist approval |
| Rights/representation gap | Missing license or disputed seller authority | Registrar / legal | Remove from publication/display/sale until resolved |
| Artwork loss/damage | Condition discrepancy, failed custody or incident | Registrar / insurer | Professional response, evidence, restricted communication and agreed claim process |
| Queue/crowd pressure | Route holding exceeds safe/pleasant operation | Visitor / security / producer | Stop admission/departures, reroute only within approvals, communicate delays/refunds |
| Medical/safeguarding | Person needing help or child-protection concern | Qualified providers / visitor lead | Follow professional plan and emergency route. Protect identities |
| Technical outage | Power, network, booking or display failure | Technical / product | Safe shutdown. Manual help/check-in/catalogue. Artist-approved fallback |
| Noise/neighbor issue | Complaint or breached approved condition | Producer / owner | Reduce/stop affected program, communicate and document correction |
| Severe weather/transport | Unsafe arrival or disrupted logistics | Producer / visitor | Actual professional/site advice. Timely practical updates and policy-compliant changes |
| Privacy/security incident | Unauthorized access/disclosure or misdirected record | Product / legal | Contain, preserve facts, professional response and required notification analysis |
| Reputational/artwashing concern | Local critique, misleading claims or unpaid contribution | Program / community / PR | Listen, correct facts, pay owed work and change harmful practice |
| Fraud/payment dispute | Unverified release request or suspicious invoice/payment | Seller / finance / registrar | Pause release, verify through known channels and agreed provider process |
| Staff burnout/absence | Coverage gaps or unsafe fatigue | Producer / program | Paid backup/shift changes, reduce program and respect rest |
| Unsustainable future promises | Automatic second-edition announcement | Governance | Evaluate real outcomes/site/cash first. No automatic renewal |

## Escalation and communication

Protect people and follow the qualified emergency/site response first. One authorized producer manages operational decisions. One authorized communications lead releases verified information. Do not speculate about cause/blame, disclose private identities or claim professional clearance that has not occurred.

Prepare actual contact tree, agency/vendor/owner routes, incident/medical/security procedures, public holding statements, refund/support instructions and backups privately. Rehearse them in July and before opening.

## Rescope principles

Keep an excellent coherent approved experience. Remove unsupported zones, expensive commissions, late-night elements or unproven products before compromising artist pay, basic service, rights or safety. A smaller version must have its own viable cost/cash model. Relocation requires new rights, access, insurance, approvals and truthful communication. A venue license is not transferable by assumption.

Pause or change the scope when an approval or funding condition fails.
