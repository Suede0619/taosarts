# Commercial, rights and operational drafting templates

**Drafting aids for professional review. Not executed contracts or a substitute for local counsel.** Copy real names, signatures, bank details, prices under negotiation and personal information into controlled private records. Do not publish them here. All dates/fees below require actual agreement.

## A. Venue license term sheet

Parties and authority. Exact address/floors/concourse and permitted use. Condition/possession. Design, install, public, strike and restoration windows. Approved capacities/hours. Construction/tenant separation. Services and responsibility. Base rent waiver if agreed. Deposits. Documented cost caps. Proposed profit-share basis/exclusions/waterfall. Budgets/related-party controls. Insurance/indemnity as reviewed. Applicable approvals. Owner/producer stop rights. Cancellation/displacement. Restoration/handback. Signage/media/data rights. Dispute/legal terms. Acceptance conditions and authorized signatures.

Explicitly define “distributable profit.” Exclude seller balances, tax, refunds, shipping pass-through and restricted funds. A 10–20% range is not an agreed rate. Agree whether the organizer reimburses services before calculating participation. Prevent duplicate charges.

## B. Paid discovery underwriting scope

Contracting entity. $75k or actual agreed fee. $70k work/$5k reserve illustration. Deliverables and acceptance. Milestone payment. Standalone value if no event. Permitted credits/recognition. Rights/confidentiality. Expenses. Actual use/earning/refund terms. Stop/cancellation. No guaranteed event or investment return. No automatic charitable deduction. Remaining budget and decision process.

## C. Sponsorship agreement / benefits schedule

Parties. Actual package/fee/cash dates. Benefit ID and deliverable. Approved marks/media/artist rights. Exclusivity boundaries. Hospitality/access capacity. Activation cost. Producer/owner approval. Data/privacy. Disclosure. Measurement/proof. Cancellation/force-majeure/service-credit/refund treatment. Use of funds. Independent curation. Responsible counterpart and close.

An in-kind contribution has description/value/method and is not cash. A sponsor acquisition of art has its own seller transaction. It is not event operating income.

## D. Gallery / seller participation

Authority/representation. Selection and scope. Accepted work list. Room and included support. Optional paid-service terms and earning/refund schedule. No purchased selection. Seller-of-record. Pricing/currency/tax/payment/shipping. Organizer commission if any expressly agreed (proposal assumes zero). Holds/returns/refunds. Condition/value/insurance. Packing/transport/install. Staff/customer care. Image/credit license. Sales-attribution evidence and privacy. Cancellation, release and final return/settlement.

## E. Direct-artist participation / commission

Artist and authorized entity. Original brief/artist freedom. Accepted deliverables. Fee/expenses and milestone schedule. Materials/fabrication/technical responsibilities. Work ownership and separate copyright. Specified license/channels/term/territory/crops/recording. Credit. Approval process. Condition/insurance. Public interaction/access. Maintenance/operator. Install/strike/return. Cancellation/kill fee. Sale terms if applicable. Final acceptance and reconciliation.

Do not make all pay contingent on unrelated art sales or a sponsor's late payment. A stipend is not an undisclosed purchase of the artist's IP.

## F. Artwork loan / custody record

Unique work ID. Owner/lender authority. Artist/title/year/medium/dimensions. Actual agreed insurance value/basis. Condition with date/photos permitted. Provenance/restrictions held privately. Permitted display/location/term. Packing/transport/custody chain. Environment/security/conservation. Reproduction rights separately. Damage/incident process. Movement/release signatures. Return condition and receipt.

A physical work loan is not artist endorsement or unrestricted reproduction permission.

## G. Speaker / educator / performer engagement

Actual session/role. Preparation, rehearsal and delivery. Fee/expenses. Paid hours/classification review. Materials/technical/access rider. Age/safeguarding requirements. Consent/recording/reuse/credit. Cancellation/travel. Backup. Authorized contact and acceptance. A research-candidate name cannot populate the public program before agreement.

## H. Production / vendor scope

Named competent lead. Exact deliverables/site boundaries. Owner/code interfaces. Drawings/rider. Crew/equipment. Insurance/qualifications. Setup/operation/strike. Inspection/acceptance. Price/tax/expenses. Milestones. Change control. Cancellation. Defects/support. Safety stop authority. Site return and evidence. Vendor provision is not automatically executive-producer or permit-holder responsibility.

## I. Asset / image / participant permission

Asset ID. Rights holder. Creator/work/subject. Purpose. Channels, territory, term, commercial/editorial distinction. Cropping/editing/AI-use permissions if any. Credit. Approvals. Withdrawal/expiry. Authorized signature/consent. Children's permissions and sensitive stories need specific appropriate review. A public event ticket is not unlimited likeness ownership.

## J. Role brief / funded engagement

Result, deliverables, actual accepted capacity, dates, fee/pay, classification review, authority, reporting/backup, conflicts, IP/data, expenses, decision limits, cancellation and acceptance. Founder equity/vesting and bonuses are separate agreements.

## K. Operational records

- **Purchase/change:** request ID, scope, reason, cost/cash timing, effect on artist/site/rights, funding source, alternatives, approvals and revised baseline.
- **Decision:** ID/date, question, evidence, authority, options, decision/conditions, owner and review trigger.
- **Incident:** ID/time/zone, verified facts, immediate professional response, responsible lead, actions/communications, restricted personal data, follow-up and closure.
- **Customer/artist issue:** reference, request/problem, consent, responsible seller/team, promise/terms, action/deadline and actual resolution.
- **Sponsor proof:** contracted benefit, actual delivery/date/evidence, exceptions, credit/refund and counterpart acceptance.
- **Settlement:** order/work IDs, seller, completed/deposit/refund status, tax/shipping separated, authorized payment route, condition/delivery evidence, outstanding claims and aggregate reporting permission.

[Data/templates](../data/templates/README.md) contains blank CSV schemas. Use them to create private records. Their presence proves no completed process or legal compliance.
