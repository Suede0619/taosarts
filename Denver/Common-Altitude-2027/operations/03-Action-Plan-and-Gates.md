# Action plan, critical path and decision gates

The [full daily plan](DAILY-ACTION-PLAN.md) covers **365 days**, November 1, 2026–October 31, 2027. The [weekly CSV](../data/weekly-action-plan.csv) covers **53 calendar weeks**, with one day in the final week. Each record identifies accountable function, output, conditional gate and status. The plan assigns tasks by role. It records no accepted commitments and grants no authority for external action.

## Project sequence

Paid discovery then site feasibility then accepted professional delivery organization then credible art/collector proposition then funded launch then rights-cleared program then authorized build then actual readiness/opening then restoration/settlement then evidence-based continuation.

Independent work can proceed in parallel. The CSV's prior-calendar-record field is navigational. It is not a claim that every workstream waits on the previous day's unrelated task. Gates and real funded dependencies control execution.

| Gate / target | Required evidence | Decision if absent |
|---|---|---|
| PRE. Nov 1–14 | Internal charter, capacity limit, accurate concept and reviewable scopes | Prepare only within authorized/affordable effort |
| G0. Nov 15 | Usable $75k discovery underwriting, accepted scopes and reserve | Do not commission unfunded work |
| G1. Dec 15 | Owner footprint/phasing/access plus qualified feasibility | Resize, alternate site or stop |
| G2. Jan 31 | Entity/authority, accepted producer/finance/collector roles and quote/contract path | Do not pretend a staffed event exists |
| G3. Mar 31 | At least $1.1m enforceable eligible non-ticket commitments **plus** usable cash/facility, cancellation and site evidence | Delay launch. Resize or stop |
| G4. May 31 | Accepted/funded/rights-cleared program, responsibility and fallback for every public promise | Remove or redesign unsupported items |
| G5. July 1 | Signed possession/use, required review path, approved technical plans and funded obligations | No build authorization |
| G6. Aug 1 | Independent operational review, staff/customer/access rehearsals | Close gaps or reduce footprint |
| G7. Aug 8 | Required final approvals/inspection, ready site/crew/rights/insurance and safe operation | Do not open unready/unsafe areas |
| G8. Oct 14 | Sixty-day reconciliation, actual liabilities and accurate report | Retain unresolved funds and continue close |

These dates set internal targets. Check grant and permit deadlines separately. The calendar moves if conditions fail. Do not preserve dates by borrowing artist money or announcing unsupported plans.

## First two weeks

Week one establishes mission, separate finance, curatorial proposition, creative direction, paid-feasibility scope and available hours. Week two turns those into a concrete seed proposition, owner evidence request, collector research method and reviewable audience-specific decks. External approaches are a later authorized human action. Creating this plan sent no messages.

## Stage outputs

Nov–Dec: discovery decision, owner/code evidence, source/rights organization and bounded scope.

Jan–Mar: delivery roles, seller/artist proposition, underwriting, quote/cash plan, prototype service and launch evidence.

Apr–May: truthful launch if authorized, actual artwork/rights, customer product and accepted learning/commission program.

Jun–Jul: travel/appointments, actual logistics, access tests, build approvals, staffing and rehearsals.

Aug: controlled build/show/strike, buyer care and daily evidence.

Sep–Oct: fulfillment, settlement, partner proof, rights/data close and continuation review.

The November 13, 2027 ninety-day claims/attribution review falls after year one. Put it on the actual controlled operational calendar when the event dates become real.

## Weekly control

Review evidence, accepted hours, critical defects, usable cash, commitments/cancellation exposure, artist/seller obligations, decisions and next deliverables. Every unresolved item has a responsible function and a date to revisit. Proposals do not silently become commitments.

Use central private operations for named people/agreements. Do not maintain a second competing commitment log in this public plan. Update the model, calendars and decks from the same inputs after a real scope decision.
