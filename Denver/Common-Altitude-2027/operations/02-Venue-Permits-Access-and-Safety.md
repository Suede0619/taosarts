# Venue, approvals, accessibility and public responsibility

**Venue and approval requirements.** The project has no engineering or legal certification. Energy Center availability remains unresolved and reported construction overlaps summer 2027. The owner must define a viable footprint before this can become an executable site plan.

## Evidence needed

Confirm the legal owner, licensee and authorized contact. Obtain addresses, floor and concourse boundaries, use and occupancy records, possession dates, construction phases, tenant rights and easements. Qualified specialists must review access, toilets, rest areas, exits, lifts, life safety, structural loads and utilities. Agree loading, storage, hours, noise, security, medical cover, insurance, restoration and cancellation.

No count of offices, building square footage, artist portfolio or earlier event elsewhere establishes assembly capacity here. A concourse is not automatically a public mall or a legally available route between towers.

## Approval matrix to complete

| Topic | Responsible professional/counterpart | Evidence before release |
|---|---|---|
| Site possession/use | Owner/property manager and counsel | Signed defined license/window, consents and construction separation |
| Use/occupancy/fire | Qualified local code/architect/fire professionals and applicable city process | Specific required submissions/reviews/inspections and safe capacity |
| Installations | Appropriate engineers/contractors and producer | Loads, rigging, power, materials/effects and maintained protected routes |
| Accessibility | Qualified access lead with site specialists and representative users | Usable physical/communication/sensory routes and emergency interfaces |
| Public realm/transport | Correct owners/agencies as applicable | Queuing, loading, sidewalk/street/RTD coordination and permits where needed |
| Food/alcohol | Appropriate licensed operators/authorities | Correct licensee/process. No commercial-event assumption of nonprofit permit eligibility |
| Security/medical | Qualified providers and producer | Actual staff, escalation, incident and emergency plan |
| Insurance/art care | Broker/insurer, registrar, owner and sellers | Required liability/art/custody/transit/worker coverage and exclusions |
| Consumer/privacy | Counsel, product and finance | Terms/refunds, consent, data controls and truthful sales/availability |
| Restoration/close | Owner/producer/registrar | Handback standard, work return and claim evidence |

Denver distinguishes the Office of Special Events process for public-property events from private indoor activity. Being outside one process does not remove building/fire/access/licensing responsibilities. Use the actual process for the actual footprint, including public-realm interfaces. Published agency lead times start after the team understands the proposed use. Allow separate time to assess whether the office space can host a large fair. ## Access standard

Provide clear arrival information, step-free routes, readable signs, seating, suitable toilets and sensory information. Visitors also need accessible booking and support, trained staff and emergency communication. Free admission alone does not make the event accessible. Site-specific professional requirements and actual user testing control. [ADA framework](https://www.ada.gov/resources/title-iii-manual/)

Visitors must have access to core art without AI, smartphones or biometrics. Provide a clear conventional route. Do not collect diagnostic details when a practical access request is sufficient. Youth/community participation needs appropriate consent/supervision and qualified policies.

## Stop conditions

Unapproved/unavailable space. Compromised life safety. Unresolved critical accessibility barrier. Unstaffed public zone. Construction conflict. Unsafe installation. Unfunded essential obligations. Missing rights or insurance. Or a provider's professional stop direction. Close the affected zone or do not open, communicate truthfully and apply agreed refund/cancellation terms.

Qualified professionals and agencies must supply the actual site drawings, opinions, permits and insurance binders. The [document register](../DOCUMENT-REGISTER.md) identifies who must supply them and by which gate.
