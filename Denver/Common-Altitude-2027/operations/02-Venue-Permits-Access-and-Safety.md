# Venue, approvals, accessibility and public responsibility

**Issue/approval plan, not engineering or legal certification.** Energy Center availability is unresolved and reported construction overlaps summer 2027. The owner must define a viable footprint before this can become an executable site plan.

## Evidence needed

Legal owner/licensee/counterpart authority; exact addresses/floors/concourse boundaries; existing use/occupancy records; construction possession/phasing; tenants and easements; accessible routes/toilets/rest; exits/stairs/lifts; fire/life-safety systems; floor/point loads; power/air/heat; loading/storage; permitted hours/noise; security/medical requirements; insurance and restoration/cancellation terms.

No count of offices, building square footage, artist portfolio or earlier event elsewhere establishes assembly capacity here. A concourse is not automatically a public mall or a legally available route between towers.

## Approval matrix to complete

| Topic | Responsible professional/counterpart | Evidence before release |
|---|---|---|
| Site possession/use | Owner/property manager and counsel | Signed defined license/window, consents and construction separation |
| Use/occupancy/fire | Qualified local code/architect/fire professionals and applicable city process | Specific required submissions/reviews/inspections and safe capacity |
| Installations | Appropriate engineers/contractors and producer | Loads, rigging, power, materials/effects and maintained protected routes |
| Accessibility | Qualified access lead with site specialists and representative users | Usable physical/communication/sensory routes and emergency interfaces |
| Public realm/transport | Correct owners/agencies as applicable | Queuing, loading, sidewalk/street/RTD coordination and permits where needed |
| Food/alcohol | Appropriate licensed operators/authorities | Correct licensee/process; no commercial-event assumption of nonprofit permit eligibility |
| Security/medical | Qualified providers and producer | Actual staff, escalation, incident and emergency plan |
| Insurance/art care | Broker/insurer, registrar, owner and sellers | Required liability/art/custody/transit/worker coverage and exclusions |
| Consumer/privacy | Counsel, product and finance | Terms/refunds, consent, data controls and truthful sales/availability |
| Restoration/close | Owner/producer/registrar | Handback standard, work return and claim evidence |

Denver distinguishes the Office of Special Events process for public-property events from private indoor activity. Being outside one process does not remove building/fire/access/licensing responsibilities. Use the actual process for the actual footprint, including public-realm interfaces. Published multi-agency timing is a minimum reference, not enough time to discover whether an office can host a large fair. ## Access standard

Clear pre-arrival information; step-free routes and alternatives; readable signs/labels; rest/seating; appropriate toilets; sensory information and options; accessible booking/customer support; trained respectful staff; and emergency communication. Free admission alone does not make the event accessible. Site-specific professional requirements and actual user testing control. [ADA framework](https://www.ada.gov/resources/title-iii-manual/)

No visitor is required to use AI, a smartphone, biometrics or a confusing route to see core art. Do not collect diagnostic details when a practical access request is sufficient. Youth/community participation needs appropriate consent/supervision and qualified policies.

## Stop conditions

Unapproved/unavailable space; compromised life safety; unresolved critical accessibility barrier; unstaffed public zone; construction conflict; unsafe installation; unfunded essential obligations; missing rights or insurance; or a provider's professional stop direction. Close the affected zone or do not open, communicate truthfully and apply agreed refund/cancellation terms.

The later site-specific drawings, licensed professional opinions, permits and insurance binders cannot be fabricated in a document package. The [document register](../DOCUMENT-REGISTER.md) identifies who must supply them and by which gate.
