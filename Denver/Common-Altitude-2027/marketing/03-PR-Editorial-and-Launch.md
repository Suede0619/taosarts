# PR, editorial and launch plan

**Working plan · October 24, 2026.** No pitches, releases or announcements have been sent. A real launch follows site/funding/rights authority. Use the actual approved facts in the release copy.

## The story

A familiar place becomes an extraordinary encounter with art—and a useful market for artists. The strongest story has specific commissioned work, credible artists/sellers, a real public welcome, a responsibly activated site and measurable paid creative contribution.

Avoid an unqualified world-first, a guaranteed sensation, borrowed Art Basel status, invented sellouts, suggested Banksy involvement or claims that two empty towers are already ours. There are close vacant-office precedents; quality and specificity must do the work.

## Media research priorities

Potential outlets to investigate include specialist art-market publications, contemporary-art journals, design/architecture media and Denver/Colorado cultural reporting. Examples: The Art Newspaper, Artnet News, Artsy editorial, Wallpaper*, Dezeen, Designboom, Colossal, Juxtapoz, Hyperallergic, Denver Westword, CPR, 5280 and The Colorado Sun.

These are editorial-fit hypotheses, not contacts, coverage commitments or paid placements. Confirm each outlet's current remit, appropriate editor/pitch route, deadlines and submission rules before an authorized approach. Do not invent journalist names or scrape private contact details. Earned coverage remains independent.

## Sequence

1. Prepare a rights-cleared fact sheet, approved statements, artist/seller credits, proposed/confirmed status, access information and a real spokesperson protocol.
2. Offer useful authorized discovery conversations where appropriate; distinguish background, embargo and on-record terms.
3. Announce only after the actual launch gate. State free/paid choices and practical access clearly.
4. Release confirmed artist/process stories, not speculative celebrity hints.
5. Provide press previews and interviews that do not disrupt art care, access or buyer privacy.
6. During the show, publish verified updates and customer information.
7. After the show, issue reconciled results and correct inaccurate claims.

## Press kit contents

Event fact sheet; approved brand assets; confirmed program/artist/seller list; licensed whole-work/detail/process images; credits/captions; owner-approved site description; public and sensory/access information; actual ticket/refund route; spokesperson roles; source/assumption sheet for numbers; media-use permissions; support contact supplied privately.

Artist biographies, photos and quotes require approval. Existing project participants are anonymous in this public repository; a private release version can add approved sender/spokesperson details after consent.

## Creative tactics worth testing

A short artist-approved process film; a beautifully made “one work, five questions” editorial series; a room-scale intervention revealed through actual making; a useful collector-care guide; public first-look hours with paid educators; owner-approved architectural storytelling; and local conversations before national promotion.

No unauthorized projection, paste-up, drone flight, trespass, street occupation or fake news stunt is part of the plan. An artistic surprise can be approved, accessible and accurately represented.

## Paid relationships and crisis communication

Influencer/creator/ambassador agreements specify work, rights, fees and clear material-connection disclosures. Do not buy a review and present it as independent editorial. [FTC disclosure guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)

For an incident: protect people, follow professional response, establish verified facts, name one authorized communications lead, avoid speculation/identity disclosure, explain service changes and provide timely corrections. The [risk plan](../operations/05-Risk-Crisis-and-Contingencies.md) assigns escalation. A sponsor cannot suppress necessary truthful public safety information.

Measure useful coverage quality, accurate message comprehension, qualified inquiries and actual referrals—not fabricated advertising-value equivalents or a guaranteed global reach number.
