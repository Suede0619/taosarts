# Campaign calendar and asset system

**Working schedule. Release dates depend on gates.** The [365-day plan](../operations/DAILY-ACTION-PLAN.md) is authoritative. This document schedules marketing work and assets. The private activity log remains the commitment record.

| Period | Audience / objective | Content and action | Owner | Release condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nov–Dec | Owner, paid advisers and discovery underwriter | Accurate concept/seed decks, research interviews and site evidence | Program / finance / producer | Authorized private discussion, funded scope |
| Jan–Mar | Appropriate artists/galleries, sponsors and qualified buyers | Individualized propositions, useful collecting research and creative tests | Artistic / commercial / collector | Correct counterpart, permissions and funded work |
| April | Controlled public awareness | Clear what/why/status, genuine accepted participants and public promise | Brand / PR | G3 plus specific announcement authority |
| May | Registration/service readiness | Free/paid explanation, real program information, tested booking/support | Product / visitor / brand | Deliverable product, terms and access evidence |
| June | Meaningful consideration | Approved work/context stories, appointments and travel/access help | Collector / editorial | Current inventory/rights and confirmed facts |
| July | Conversion and preparation | Reminders, program choices, arrival and sensory/access information | Product / visitor / PR | Current approved capacity and service readiness |
| Aug 1–8 | Final readiness | Confirmations, changes, useful practical content | Producer / customer care | Actual readiness. No pressure to hide changes |
| Aug 9–15 | Informed participation | Daily program/support information, permitted art/process stories | Program / PR / visitor | Real-time facts, privacy and rights |
| Aug 16–Oct 31 | Fulfillment and evidence | Buyer care, seller follow-up, sponsor proof and verified outcomes | Collector / finance / program | Completion/consent and reconciled aggregates |
| Nov 13 | Ninety-day close follow-through | Claims/reserve and later-sales review | Finance / legal | Actual obligations reviewed |

Suggested owned cadence after authorized launch: one useful editorial/email unit weekly, two to four distinct social units weekly where there is genuine content, practical updates as needed and daily factual show information. Do not generate filler or flood consented lists merely to meet a quota.

## Minimum asset register

| Asset | Required fields | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Concept/owner/underwriter decks | Version, audience, conditional ask, sources and finance | Same current numbers and status |
| Brand masters | Mark/wordmark, colors/type/layout rules, source/license | Editable originals and reproduction proofs |
| Artwork package | Work ID, whole/detail images, caption/credit, license, seller and availability | Accurate, rights-cleared and current |
| Artist/process story | Accepted participant, scope, quote/image approval, channels/term | Actual story. No invented endorsement |
| Public information | What/when/site status, free/paid, arrival/access/sensory, terms/support | Representative user can plan a visit |
| Program cards | Session, lead, age/access, price, capacity, booking state | Accepted funded delivery and correct live state |
| Collector materials | Work/appointment/quote/delivery records | Privacy and seller authority |
| Sponsor proof | Contracted benefit, evidence, delivery date and exceptions | Reconciled, no fabricated reach |
| Post-event report | Definitions, methods, actual completed results and limits | Approved aggregates only |

The [concept poster and mark](../assets/README.md) are original review assets. They are not a venue announcement or a license to reproduce another artist's work. Production/publication waits for the applicable gates.
