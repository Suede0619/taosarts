# Creative production briefs and acceptance standards

**Working scopes, October 24, 2026.** Commission work only after funding, agreed capacity and the relevant gates. No designer, photographer, artist or agency has accepted these assignments.

## Initial brand/design commission

**Owner:** brand/design lead. **Inputs:** this brand book, approved concept, decision register, real audience research and confirmed site/status facts. **Scope:** one primary identity direction, documented refinements, presentation components, initial application proofs and production-ready vector masters. The recommended direction is already supplied. The designer’s scope covers assessment and development of this direction. Set a limit on concept rounds.

The scope covers mark refinements, type, colors, spacing, artwork labels, wayfinding prototypes and a deck master. It also covers mobile artwork cards, invitations, sponsor labels, access and reduced-motion rules, a licensed-asset register and editable source. Acceptance: coherent at small/large size, readable facts, no rights gaps, consistent free/paid/status messaging, user-tested comprehension and practical production methods.

The seed model allows $7,500 for a defined discovery brand scope. It does not buy all campaign execution, a full production website, every physical sign or unlimited rounds. Later brand work has its own funded scope within proposed team/content allocations.

## Art direction and photography

Create a capture specification before collecting hundreds of images:
- Whole artwork and detail. Exact work ID. Dimensions/orientation. Color-managed master where possible. Approved crop/credit. Proof of rights.
- Portrait/interview permissions, accurate captions and no invented relationship.
- Space imagery only from owner-approved access. Clearly distinguish existing conditions, commissioned art and conceptual renderings.
- Separate press-ready editorial files from commercial promotional licenses.
- Name files with work or asset IDs. Keep buyer identities out of filenames. Sensitive provenance/condition documents remain controlled.

The image set must let buyers assess art accurately on phones and larger screens. Credits must survive export. Rights must cover the intended media, territory and term. Images must show size and condition clearly.

## Campaign content system

Build a small repeatable series:
1. **A Room Is Becoming…**. Publish an artist-approved process story only after the parties agree to participation and rights.
2. **How to Look / How to Buy**. Intelligible, useful collector education.
3. **The People Who Make This Place**. Paid/consented local stories.
4. **One Work, Five Questions**. Artist, material, scale, price/context and living with it.
5. **The Week Opens Up**. Confirmed practical access, free/paid choices and appointments.

Each asset has owner, audience, objective, CTA, source/permission, format, publish condition and measurement. No “mystery artist” tease that implies an unconfirmed celebrity. A conceptual campaign can intrigue without fabricating a commission.

## Physical design and signage

Prepare a surveyed schedule of signs after site review: arrival, free route, paid reservations, accessible routes, toilets, lifts, quiet space, learning, purchase help and exit interfaces. The producer/code lead controls safety placement and material/fire requirements. Test labels and maps at their intended reading distance under actual lighting.

Use temporary reversible methods approved by the owner. Salvaged office material can be a creative resource only after clearance, condition review and removal of confidential tenant information. Do not install unapproved objects in egress paths.

## Digital/commerce design

Acceptance includes usable non-chat browsing, accurate availability, seller identity, accessible forms, full-cost quote/invoice, confirmed time-slot state, clear error/recovery and a human handoff. Never show a real “buy” button pointing at a simulated checkout. Prototype work and real inventory are visibly different.

## Presentation production

Seven audience-specific decks are in [decks](../decks/README.md). They share the identity but have different asks. Keep financial numbers generated from the project/model inputs. Use original vector diagrams in place of unlicensed artwork or misleading star-artist images.

The designed proposal supports a conditional decision at this stage. A public-facing launch deck requires confirmed sender/contact, venue/date wording, participant permissions, actual team credits and the release checklist. Editable PowerPoint is the working handoff. PDF is the visual reference. Adobe Express, Canva, Figma and Keynote can support further design work. The delivered files need no paid service or connected account.

## Production QA

Check spelling/status/dates. Source every factual number. Inspect 16:9 slide bounds and actual PDF pages. Verify links, credits, contrast and reading order. Show at least a cover, financial slide, room/program diagram and closing ask at presentation size. Test mobile/print applications separately. Obtain actual accessibility and production sign-off before launch.
