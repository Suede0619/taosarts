# COMMON ALTITUDE 2027

## OFFICE OF THE IMPOSSIBLE

**A proposed Denver art fair with sales, commissions and free public exhibitions.**

Comprehensive working creative, business and execution package prepared **October 24, 2026**. This moves beyond the prior research-only stage into the plans and presentations requested. It does not book the event, commit funds, recruit partners or announce dates.

**Recommended working format:** August 9–15, 2027. Two professional/preview days and five public art days. **Recommended planning scope:** 60 programmed rooms/equivalent spaces, with 24- and 120-space alternatives. Hundreds of rooms, both towers and the concourse remain the ambition to investigate. Not an available or permitted footprint. Names, dates, costs and demand require validation.

[Finance and cash details](business/03-Finance-and-Funding.md) explain why seller sales are not organizer cash. The main proposal assumes zero organizer art commission. The project has no demand evidence for optional gallery services or paid programs. Public grants are $0 in the base plan. The venue 10–20% basis remains a discussion hypothesis. The proposal promises no owner payout.

The model shows a cash gap before its July peak. Secure financing or reschedule work before that first gap. A $75,000 discovery commitment does not finance the whole event. The smaller alternative currently loses money. Revise its costs and revenue assumptions before selecting it.

## What is deliberately still conditional

The project still needs owner authority, a site survey, construction separation, possession terms, approved use and capacity, professional plans, quotes, contracts and insurance. It also needs artist rights, available work, accepted partners, buyer demand, financing, tax and consumer terms, and brand clearance.

The [document register](DOCUMENT-REGISTER.md) identifies the real documents/evidence still required. Templates are not signed agreements or issued permits. The decks support a discussion of the proposal. They provide no evidence of site approval, funding or partner commitments.

Public documents remain anonymous for current participants. A local ignored owner-deck copy and named-role map are available privately. They are not on GitHub. Raw sources and confidential operational records are also ignored. No email, outreach, application, booking, hiring, expenditure or website deployment occurred.

November 1, 2026 remains project day one. Taos-only website v1, its zero direct-artist commission, the existing prototype and the older models remain unchanged. This larger proposed Denver scope has its own explicit inputs and model.
