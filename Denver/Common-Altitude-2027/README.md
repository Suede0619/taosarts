# COMMON ALTITUDE 2027

## OFFICE OF THE IMPOSSIBLE

**A remarkable place to discover and acquire art, open to the city.**

Comprehensive working creative, business and execution package prepared **October 24, 2026**. This moves beyond the prior research-only stage into the plans and presentations requested. It does not book the event, commit funds, recruit partners or announce dates.

**Recommended working format:** August 9–15, 2027; two professional/preview days and five public art days. **Recommended planning scope:** 60 programmed rooms/equivalent spaces, with 24- and 120-space alternatives. Hundreds of rooms, both towers and the concourse remain the ambition to investigate—not an available or permitted footprint. Names, dates, costs and demand require validation.

[Finance and cash details](business/03-Finance-and-Funding.md) explain why seller sales are not organizer cash. The main proposal assumes zero organizer art commission. Optional gallery services and paid programs are unproven; public grants are $0 in the base plan. The venue 10–20% basis remains a discussion hypothesis; no owner payout is promised.

The first after-hold cash gap occurs before the July peak, so financing or work rephasing is needed early. A $75,000 discovery commitment does not finance the whole event. The smaller alternative currently loses money and must be redesigned before selection.

## What is deliberately still conditional

Exact owner/approver authority; surveyed footprint; construction separation and possession; approved public use/capacity; site-specific professional plans; quotes; contracts/insurance; artist/work rights and availability; candidate willingness; buyer demand; actual financing; tax/consumer treatment; name clearance and final brand approval.

The [document register](DOCUMENT-REGISTER.md) identifies the real documents/evidence still required. Templates are not signed agreements or issued permits. The decks are designed proposals suitable for a conditional discussion, not evidence that these conditions have been met.

Public documents remain anonymous for current participants. A local ignored owner-deck copy and named-role map are available privately; they are not on GitHub. Raw sources and confidential operational records are also ignored. No email, outreach, application, booking, hiring, expenditure or website deployment occurred.

November 1, 2026 remains project day one. Taos-only website v1, its zero direct-artist commission, the existing prototype and the older models remain unchanged. This larger proposed Denver scope has its own explicit inputs and model.
