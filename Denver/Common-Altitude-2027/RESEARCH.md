# Research and evidence register

**Cut-off: October 24, 2026.** This package builds on the [preceding Denver research](../Summer-2027-Fair-Research/README.md), which remains a dated source. This register and [the CSV](data/source-index.csv) list new partner and context sources.

There are **97 current indexed source URLs**: 72 partner references and 25 context/professional references. The private capture contains 98 retrieval records, including an initial incorrect DAVA domain preserved as an explicit failed attempt before correction to davarts.org. Of those requests, 88 returned HTTP 200. Nine returned non-200 bodies and one failed without a body. Web-reader extracts and earlier dated source material supplement blocked pages. HTTP 200 does not prove complete dynamic/paywalled content or current availability.

The project has contacted none of the candidates. Official portfolios and rosters show published work. They establish no interest, stock availability, fees, touring offer or endorsement. Artist and gallery entries can concern the same representation network. 72 records are not 72 independent commercial commitments. Global galleries appear in the worldwide strategy group even where they have North American offices.

The source register corrects the first DAVA domain to the address on its official About page. TRANSFER currently describes an artist-owned/archive/cooperative direction. Confirm how TRANSFER currently operates before proposing commercial participation. Pest Control's official FAQ controls over unofficial Banksy websites. The research makes no attempt to identify Banksy.

Current event calendar checks support Cherry Creek July 2–4, Cheesman Park July 24–25, Denver Arts Festival May 29–30 and Art Basel Basel June 17–20, 2027. The proposed August week still needs full local/regional/hotel/collector and owner clearance. The calendar leaves unverified 2027 Aspen and Santa Fe dates open.

Financial, attendance, price and fee inputs remain proposal assumptions. The project has no evidence that it can achieve them. Law, standards and provider guidance identify issues for review. Actual contracts, site opinions and software integrations require separate work. Exact site, capacity, construction availability, rights, quotes and actual team/funding remain unresolved.

Raw HTML/PDF, hashes, tool extracts, confidential intake, named-team mapping and private owner-deck copy remain ignored. Public deck assets contain no third-party art, images or marks. Audio/video, issued permits, title records, seller ledgers and nonpublic company underwriting were not recovered or audited.
