Working designed proposal · October 24, 2026. No site/date, partner, funding or capacity commitment. This slide script accompanies the editable PowerPoint and PDF.

## 1. COMMON ALTITUDE

TEAM + CO-PRODUCER DISCUSSION

## 2. The founding functions.

Proposed roles—not proof of accepted hours or production capacity.

- Artistic + relationships — Curatorial thesis, artists, galleries and market credibility.
- Product + program — Digital/service requirements, evidence and cross-functional coordination.
- Brand + design — Positioning, identity and coherent physical/digital communication.

## 3. The first essential additions.

Competence and actual availability matter more than a celebrity name.

- Executive producer — Comparable complex-venue delivery; authority and stop rights.
- Collector lead — Qualified demand, appointments, seller conversion and service.
- Finance/controller — Cash, restrictions, contracts, reconciliation and independent challenge.

## 4. Pay for defined work. Add upside thoughtfully.

The model is funded professional scopes with optional defined performance/profit participation.

- No indefinite unpaid-production expectation.
- Classification, fees, rights, milestones and cancellation agreed.
- Founder equity and an event bonus are different matters.
