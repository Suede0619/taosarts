Working designed proposal · October 24, 2026. No site/date, partner, funding or capacity commitment. This slide script accompanies the editable PowerPoint and PDF.

## 1. COMMON ALTITUDE

OFFICE OF THE IMPOSSIBLE
Denver Art Fair & Exhibition
Proposed August 9–15, 2027


## 2. Give the address a cultural life.

A bounded art activation designed to serve the project and the people who make art.

- A reason to visit — Exceptional work and artist-authored rooms create a compelling destination.
- A useful market — Collectors meet trusted sellers, with time, context and dependable service.
- A public welcome — Free core art discovery and paid local creative participation.

## 3. The ordinary becomes extraordinary.

An office complex becomes an art fair, exhibition, workshop and place to encounter what is possible.

- COMMON ALTITUDE is the master brand.
- OFFICE OF THE IMPOSSIBLE is the proposed first-edition theme.
- Artist authorship and buying confidence lead the experience.

## 4. One coherent edition. Room to grow.

60 programmed rooms or equivalent spaces is the proposed core—not a surveyed floor plan.

- 24 — Gallery presentations
- 12 — Direct-artist salons
- 6 — Signature briefs
- 18 — Learning, common and support

## 5. A week with a clear rhythm.

Two professional/preview days. Five public art days. All dates and spaces remain proposed.

- MON–TUE — Orientation, advisers, press and artist/collector previews.
- WED–FRI — Free art discovery, guided journeys, conversations and making.
- SAT–SUN — Public and family discovery, considered purchases and a strong close.

## 6. The demand case to prove.

Targets are unvalidated; visitors, qualified households and completed purchases are different measures.

- 12,000 — Unique visitor target
- 600 — Qualified collector households
- 150 — Completed purchasing households
- $3m — Completed art GMV target

## 7. Sell art with confidence.

Zero organizer art commission is proposed. Seller/gallery terms and external costs remain separate.

- Selected work — Independent curation, accurate information and rights-cleared images.
- Trusted sellers — Seller authority, clear invoices, condition and provenance.
- Real follow-through — Appointments, delivery coordination and completion records.

## 8. The organizer model.

Illustrative core proposal; no funding, fees or demand is validated.

- $1,815,000 — Organizer income
- $1,550,000 — Cost envelope
- $265,000 — Before owner / tax / reserves

## 9. A precise host arrangement.

A discussion structure, not an agreed venue offer.

- Space — Waived base rent for an approved footprint and possession window.
- Services — Documented incremental services with budget, responsibility and cost control.
- Participation — 10–20% of defined distributable event profit; model comparison uses 15%.

## 10. An honest owner-return illustration.

Art GMV is seller money. A share of distributable profit produces a much smaller amount.

- $265,000 — Surplus before owner, tax and reserves
- $48,500 — Initially eligible after illustrative holds
- $7,275 — 15% initial owner-share comparison
- $15,750 — Cumulative comparison only if claims hold is fully released

## 11. Serve the rental and leasing strategy.

Current published direction is rental housing plus retained office use; confirm the specific phase with the owner.

- Destination identity — An excellent cultural experience and credible public narrative.
- Qualified interest — Separate opt-in residential/commercial inquiries, with agreed follow-up.
- Construction protection — No unsupported whole-building access or schedule interference.

## 12. Fund the evidence. Then fund delivery.

Illustrative funding needs, not commitments or an investment offering.

## 13. Build a team that can deliver.

Founding artistic, product/program and brand functions are identified; appointments and capacity require agreement.

- Executive producer — Experienced delivery, site and city interfaces; role open.
- Collector + finance — Qualified buyer development and independent cash/settlement control.
- Professional specialists — Code/access, registrar/logistics, technical, visitor, security and medical.

## 14. A good neighbor. A controlled project.

The reported construction period overlaps summer 2027. Availability must be established, not assumed.

- Approved use — Actual footprint, occupancy, egress, access and inspections.
- Defined boundaries — Tenants, contractors, concourse ownership and possession.
- Public accountability — Paid artists, access, local coordination and truthful funding claims.

## 15. Six weeks to a useful decision.

Proposed discovery structure; no full event commitment is requested at this stage.

- 1 — Owner access, plans and responsible counterparts.
- 2–3 — Producer/code review, market tests and scope alternatives.
- 4–5 — Quotes, commercial terms, cash and cancellation analysis.
- 6 — Joint proceed / resize / relocate / stop decision.
