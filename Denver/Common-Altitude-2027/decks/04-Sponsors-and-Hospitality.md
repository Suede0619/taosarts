# Commercial sponsors, hospitality and destination partners

Working designed proposal, October 24, 2026. No site/date, partner, funding or capacity commitment. This slide script accompanies the editable PowerPoint and PDF.

## 1. COMMON ALTITUDE

SPONSORSHIP AND DESTINATION PARTNERSHIPS
A proposed Denver art fair and public program.


## 2. Three audience groups

The proposal serves collectors, residents and artists. Audience sizes remain targets.

- Collectors + advisers. Qualified relationships and thoughtful acquisition service.
- Residents + first buyers. Free core art discovery and intelligible participation.
- Creative community. Fees for artists, teaching and public programs.

## 3. Audience targets

The project has no measured event audience to offer sponsors.

- 12,000. Unique visitor target
- 600. Qualified collector-household target
- 5. Public art days

## 4. Program sponsorship options

Price each activity and obtain site, artist and rights approval before offering it.

- Public access + learning. Pay educators, interpretation and an excellent welcome.
- Artist work. Fund artist-authored commissions and credible context.
- Collecting + hospitality. Useful approved client visits and informed conversations.

## 5. Sponsorship packages

The $1.25m target assumes sales of these proposed packages. No sponsor has bought one.

- 1 × $400k. Presenting underwriting
- 2 × $200k. Lead program underwriting
- 4 × $75k. Program partnerships
- 10 × $15k. Supporting partnerships

## 6. Contracted benefits and evidence

Assign a responsible person, cost, rights, capacity and delivery measure to each benefit.

- Association. Approved naming/credit and appropriate content.
- Experience. Scoped client/public program and licensed hospitality.
- Evidence. Verified delivery and consented aggregate reporting.

## 7. Editorial independence and privacy

Sponsor terms must protect artist selection, image rights and visitor information.

- No bought curation. Sponsors do not purchase artist selection.
- No borrowed fame. Artist images, marks and participation need permission.
- No visitor-list sale. Privacy and consent control relationship information.

## 8. Proposed event week

August 9–15, 2027 remains unbooked. The program and site need approval.

- PREVIEW. Adviser, artist, press and partner attention.
- PUBLIC. Free art, making, conversation and guided options.
- AFTER. Seller fulfillment, sponsor evidence and qualified follow-up.

## 9. Sponsor reporting

Report actual delivery, participation and artist outcomes against the contract.

- Delivery. Actual rights/program fulfillment and service quality.
- Participation. Entries, estimated unique visitors and program use, with methods.
- Artist outcomes. Paid work, completed sales and cleared aggregate seller outcomes.

## 10. Discovery support

A contract may count discovery support once toward a later sponsorship package.

- $75,000. Paid discovery target
- 6 weeks. Proposed evidence stage
- G3. Public launch only after funding/site evidence

## 11. Commercial terms

Each partnership needs payment, cancellation, exclusivity, rights and approval terms.

- Terms. Cash schedule, cancellation, exclusivity and approved rights.
- Cost. Price and fund each program and hospitality benefit.
- Approval. Obtain owner, artist and specialist permissions.

## 12. Partnership discussion

Identify the sponsor’s objective and a specific program it can fund.

- Agree the audience and useful outcome.
- Scope rights, cost and delivery evidence.
- Commit only after the required site and funding gates.

## 13. Sources and open assumptions

No sponsor, hospitality partner or artist has accepted participation.

- Package prices and audience targets are original unvalidated assumptions.
- Primary research, business plan, marketing plan and cash model accompany the deck.
- Approved images/marks and actual agreement details belong in the release copy.
