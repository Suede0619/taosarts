## Release and decision method

A decision record states question, evidence, options, authority, conditions, responsible function and review trigger. Real names/signatures/terms stay private. A role suggestion is not acceptance; an interested partner is not contracted; an approved budget is not collected cash.

Before releasing a deck, confirm audience, sender, actual status, financial version, rights, permitted participant names/marks, contact route and next decision. Owner/underwriter discovery decks can honestly support an initial conditional discussion. A public launch/collector catalogue needs the later site/funding/inventory gates.

Changes to scope, date, fees, revenue, cash restrictions or program flow update project inputs, generated outputs, decks and relevant prose together. Earlier research remains a dated source, not a competing current plan.
