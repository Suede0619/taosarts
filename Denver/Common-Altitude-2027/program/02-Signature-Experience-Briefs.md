# Signature experience briefs: first-edition proposals

**October 24, 2026.** Commission and fund the artist’s interpretation, then obtain the required approvals. Names below are research candidates or reference capabilities, never selected artists. Six artistic-fee envelopes total $120,000. Technical envelopes total $200,000. Test these budget limits against actual quotes. Named candidates have supplied no prices.

## 1. The Building Listens

**Experience:** a room becomes a responsive composition through an optional, simple artist-designed interaction. It might use a button, movement within a clearly marked area or another nonintrusive control. The work should remain meaningful for someone who chooses not to interact.

**Artistic question:** how can individual attention become a shared experience? Commission an original response. The brief grants no right to copy an existing pulse or light installation.

**Potential capabilities:** Denver Digerati/local media artists. Android Jones or NONOTAK for an appropriate scoped collaboration. Moment Factory for technical discovery where funded. A major named studio may be unaffordable within this envelope.

**Envelope:** $30,000 artistic / $70,000 technical. **Lead:** artistic director + technical producer. **Acceptance:** signed rights/rider, safe materials/power, reduced-sensory/noninteractive access, staffed recovery, no undisclosed recording or biometric capture. **Fallback:** a pre-rendered artist-approved composition on freestanding approved equipment.

## 2. The Office of Small Miracles

**Experience:** exceptional editions/small works receive careful light, context, visible prices and approachable purchase help. A first buyer can ask basic questions without embarrassment. Keep quality and provenance as strong as in expensive rooms.

**Potential capabilities:** Hecho a Mano, selected Denver galleries and independent print/edition artists. Invite artists to develop their own presentation. Confirm stock separately.

**Envelope:** $15,000 artistic / $15,000 technical. **Lead:** art-market/registrar + brand. **Acceptance:** rights-cleared works, honest edition information, seller-led invoices, packing/transport information and a genuinely usable non-digital option. **Fallback:** a beautifully edited physical print/edition cabinet with human support.

## 3. The Department of Possible Futures

**Experience:** artists transform cleared, safe office materials and their own work into propositions about ways of living/working together. Label fictional content. No abandoned tenant documents or private data become raw material.

**Potential capabilities:** paid local artists through an agreed process. Black Cube/Factory Obscura as possible curatorial/production advisers. Artists may propose materially different approaches.

**Envelope:** $25,000 artistic / $40,000 technical. **Lead:** artistic/community + producer. **Acceptance:** artist authorship, local involvement, materials/fire/structural review, no sensitive records and reversible installation. **Fallback:** freestanding sculpture, drawings and texts that achieve the idea without construction.

## 4. The Slow Room

**Experience:** a quiet room for visitors to spend time with material, image, sound or light. It should be artistically compelling and allow seated, low-stimulation looking.

**Potential capabilities:** a scoped local/material artist, a gallery loan, or an appropriate artist-approved existing work. Ann Hamilton or similarly experienced practitioners are longer-lead possibilities only if scope/rights/budget permit.

**Envelope:** $20,000 artistic / $25,000 technical. **Lead:** artistic + access. **Acceptance:** comfortable access, clear sensory information, conservation and quiet conditions, no imitation of a famous artist's room. **Fallback:** a curated loan of smaller works with a careful artist-approved sound/text element.

## 5. The City Makes Its Portrait

**Experience:** paid local artists work with willing people to make a portrait of creative life. Photographic, drawn, spoken or materially interpreted. Ask participants how to credit them and whether they permit later promotional use.

**Potential capabilities:** local photographers/artists, CPAC, community organizations. JR/Inside Out only through their actual approval process and within its terms. An Inside Out action would not imply JR attends or endorses the commercial fair.

**Envelope:** $15,000 artistic / $20,000 technical. **Lead:** community + commissioned artist + privacy/rights owner. **Acceptance:** meaningful participation, compensation, appropriate consent, safeguarding and participant control. **Fallback:** a smaller paid local portrait series with cleared display rights.

## 6. The Living Catalogue

**Experience:** a physical/digital reading room where people understand art, ask a knowledgeable person and find available works across the event. Art records, books, material samples where appropriate and appointments make looking actionable.

**Potential capabilities:** accepted sellers, experienced registrar/collector staff and the product/brand team. An optional carefully tested search/concierge can help, but the room works without AI.

**Envelope:** $15,000 artistic/editorial / $30,000 technical. **Lead:** product/collector/registrar. **Acceptance:** accurate current seller information, good images, accessible reading, privacy and reliable inquiry-to-seller handoff. **Fallback:** printed controlled catalogue, updated availability sheets and trained staff.

## Procurement and control

For each brief, request artist/studio-authored scope, relevant work, total fee/expenses, rights, rider, fabrication/transport, staffing, accessibility, site dependencies, maintenance, setup/strike, cancellation and fallback. Ask whether the artist’s scope can fit the budget.

Prefer one exceptional achievable anchor over six weak expensive simulations. If quotes exceed the combined $320,000 envelopes, reduce/reframe works, use appropriate loans or secure separately approved money. Do not remove artist pay to save an unpriced technical concept.

Commission milestones: accepted brief/site conditions then funded design then approved technical/rights review then fabrication/install then acceptance/rehearsal then operation then strike/return then final reconciliation. No final fee is contingent on how photogenic the work is or whether an unrelated sponsor pays late.

None of the briefs grants permission to reproduce existing works, use artist names in public promotion, alter owner property or collect visitors' personal data.
