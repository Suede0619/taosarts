# Finance, underwriting and cash control

**Working proposal, October 24, 2026.** All values are assumptions in USD. Read [generated results](../data/financial-results.md) and [monthly cash CSV](../data/monthly-cash.csv). The prior Denver $750,000 sensitivity was a smaller unpriced illustration. This proposed 60-space edition uses a new $1,550,000 envelope. The repository retains the earlier research and model.

## Six financial measures

Track six measures separately: completed art GMV, seller receipts, organizer revenue, operating costs, accounting surplus and distributable cash. Artwork deposits, tax, shipping, refunds and restricted grants are separate. Record financing proceeds as liabilities or capital. Exclude them from operating revenue. Record in-kind support separately from cash sponsorship. It cannot pay cash expenses.

The core scenario: $3m completed-art target. $1.815m organizer income. $1.55m costs. $265,000 before owner, tax and reserves. No organizer art commission and no public grant income.

The package prices are proposals. The project has no market survey supporting them. Every benefit has an owner, cost, rights, capacity, evidence method and fallback. The parties may agree to credit the $75,000 seed contract toward a later package. The model includes that amount once within the $1.25m target. The discovery deliverables must have standalone value and stop/cancellation terms. An underwriter is not promised an event irrespective of feasibility.

## Cash timing and facility

The monthly model reserves all customer advance receipts until performance, a provisional sponsor-cancellation hold and phase-appropriate operating cash. The model needs **$530,000 of bridge financing in July**. Unrestricted sponsor cash reduces that requirement to $215,000. Sponsor contracts and actual earning/refund obligations control. Neither assumption is a legal determination.

Seek a professionally structured facility or equivalent usable backstop of up to $600,000, with actual pricing, draw/repayment and cancellation rules. The first modeled after-hold gap appears in **December 2026**, well before the July peak: secure the necessary early coverage or retime/reduce work before committing. The $75,000 seed funds discovery. Later expenses need additional usable cash. This is not an approved loan or securities offering. Its fees/interest need quotes and may require higher professional/finance allowance. Artist balances and restricted awards cannot bridge production.

Before any commitment: usable unrestricted cash plus enforceable collectible receipts available before the next payment must cover approved spend, refunds/cancellation exposure and reserves. An unsigned sponsor conversation is zero funded runway. A signed contract is still not cash.

## Conditional owner waterfall

Suggested negotiation: waived base rent, documented services/cost cap and 10–20% of defined distributable profit. Model comparison uses 15%.

Starting core surplus $265,000 less model tax cash reserve $60,000, operating reserve $100,000 and customer-claim hold $56,500 leaves $48,500 initially eligible. A hypothetical 15% owner amount is $7,275. A claim-free later release produces a cumulative $15,750 comparison. These are not promised payouts. Actual liabilities, tax, financing terms and agreed priority may change every amount.

Do not market $3m art sales as $3m available to the organizer or owner. If the owner requires a guaranteed minimum, price/fund it explicitly and rerun downside. Actual founder fees compensate work before optional upside. Participants have agreed no equity or profit split.

## Actual seed routes and exclusions

Commercial underwriting, patrons using a clearly defined lawful payment structure, a developer activation contribution and experienced co-production are the first routes to test. No party has committed. Space and cash can be separate contributions. Do not presume the developer will or will not supply either.

Public grant base income is zero. Current DDA arts scope is narrow. DENVER CREATES' reviewed round closed. The city has paused PSYAH. Bonfils-Stanton generally excludes events. The prior [funding research](../../Summer-2027-Fair-Research/05-Team-Compensation-and-Seed-Funding.md) provides dated eligibility/links. A genuine eligible charitable component must have its own accepted applicant, purpose, agreement, accounting and reporting.

## Financial operations

Use a controlled event account/ledger, documented authorization limits, purchase orders, contract/rights evidence, change log and dual review for cash movements. Seller-led invoices/payment routes remain separate. Track sponsor earning/refund status and service deliverables.

Daily during the show: reconcile event cash/tickets, record completed seller-reported orders with deduplication, review disputes and maintain customer/artist support. Weekly before/after: update dated commitments, cash restrictions, receivables and forecast. After closing: settle legitimate work and liabilities before considering distributions.

The November 13, 2027 ninety-day reserve review is after the year-one calendar. A clean bank balance does not automatically authorize a payout.
