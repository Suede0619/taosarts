# Governance, artist economics and durable benefit

**Proposed operating principles · October 24, 2026.** No entity, ownership split, compensation agreement or festival authority is established by this plan.

## Decision rights

A small accountable founding group sets mission and budget direction. The executive producer owns integrated delivery and site operations; qualified specialists retain authority over safety/access/engineering; artistic direction owns selection with recusals; finance controls usable cash and settlement; brand/product own their defined domains. Use a written approval matrix and private accepted-role register.

Before significant contracts: establish legal entity/counterpart authority, brand/IP rights, founder ownership/vesting and departure terms, related-party fee approval, banking/signatory controls, insurance, data responsibilities and dispute/escalation processes. An event profit share is not company equity.

## Artist proposition

Core direct-artist participation is proposed free; commissioned artists/educators are paid for accepted work. Organizer art commission is proposed at zero. Galleries retain their own disclosed representation relationships; no promise overrides them. Payment-processing/tax/shipping/insurance responsibilities must be explicit.

Twelve proposed direct-artist salons share a $30,000 stipend envelope; selection is independent of payment. This is a budget hypothesis, not an open grant or guarantee. Do not recruit unpaid production labor under the artist-opportunity banner.

Use clear artist/loan/consignment terms: scope, fee/expenses, work authority, rights, credits, condition/value, installation/handling, sales responsibility, cancellation, return and settlement. Funds owed to artists/sellers are protected from organizer cash needs.

## Independence and culture

Disclose conflicts. A gallery adviser recuses from favoring represented artists. A sponsor does not select artists or suppress legitimate content by default. Cultural representation is artist/community-led; Indigenous, Latin American or local identity is not decorative theme material. Seek relevant permission and paid advice.

Institutions are not content warehouses. Museum/education participation requires mission/authority review, separate artist rights and appropriate fees. A local public consultation is not a substitute for consent from the people represented.

## Public benefit and legacy

Free core art discovery; paid local learning/creative work; first-buyer support; accessible routes; useful artist/buyer relationships; and evidence of completed earnings. These are the first-edition legacy promises to resource and measure.

Possible additional acquisition funds, grants, studio-support programs or year-round commissions need separate lawful funding, governance and accounting. They are not included as automatic deductions from art sales or invented tax-deductible donations. A sponsor's art purchase is an art transaction, not event operating income; count completed GMV once and preserve independent selection.

## Compensation and upside

Defined funded scopes first, optional properly defined upside later. Founder service fees, ownership, staff pay, vendor fees, sponsorship-sales incentives and venue participation have distinct bases. Avoid stacking percentages against undefined “proceeds.”

The current core-fee and waterfall illustrations are in the [finance plan](03-Finance-and-Funding.md). No founder split is agreed. Appropriate labor/tax/legal advice determines actual classification and distributions. Do not treat an employee as a volunteer or contractor merely because the plan is early-stage. [Colorado classification guidance](https://cdle.colorado.gov/sites/cdle/files/info_%2310_who_is_and_isn%27t_an_employee_08.11.25.pdf)

## Accountability

Publish only cleared aggregate outcomes: completed art value, artist fees, distribution of sales outcomes, seller/customer service, access, participation, incidents and financial viability. Keep personal records private. Record failures and corrections as well as successes.

A future edition is earned through evidence. It does not follow automatically from a brand launch, a large crowd or an ambitious deck.
