# Southwest Art: platform strategy and starter brand brief

**October 23, 2026, Anonymous working brief for review.** Current planning direction: the year-round art and destination platform is the core business. Festivals and events support discovery, relationships and visits. This updates earlier festival-led framing. It is not an approved budget, a legal entity decision, a festival-board resolution or a funded design commission.

**Project day one: November 1, 2026. Taos is the only v1 region.** The existing prototype is a design demonstration. Launch timing depends on permissions, funding, service readiness and review.

## The business and the promise

Help artists sell enough work to keep living and working in Taos. Give a remote buyer confidence that the art is real, available, accurately represented and supported by reliable human service. Give a visitor an edited reason to come and a useful path through art, galleries, experiences, food and lodging. Make residents and first buyers feel welcome.

“Southwest” identifies geography. The selection includes several artistic styles. Contemporary, experimental, historic and other work can belong when selection, context and rights support it. Avoid a generic tourist directory or a visual identity that reduces the region’s cultures to decoration. Cultural authority and representation require informed local participation.

The year-round website and buying/visit service should build repeat relationships. Events can attract people into that service and provide artist/public value. Neither attendance nor prestige alone pays an artist. Measure completed art sales, artist proceeds, repeat buyers, service quality and sustainable unrestricted operating cash. Keep event and platform entities, permissions and finances distinct until actual agreements establish their relationship.

## Audience and experience

| Audience | What must feel convincing | Essential experience |
|---|---|---|
| Collector or remote buyer | Artistic quality, context, provenance and dependable service | Accurate whole-artwork images. Clear price/availability. Informed questions. Viewing/full-cost quote. Reliable purchase and delivery. |
| First buyer or resident | Welcome, intelligible language and choice within a real budget | Prices and useful filters without embarrassment. Public discovery, understandable stories and easy human help. |
| Artist or gallery | Fair treatment, representation control and useful demand | Clear terms, rights/credits, accurate inventory, qualified inquiries and accountable fulfillment/payout. |
| Visitor | A coherent art-centered reason to travel | Selective practical options, honest travel/access information and a short workable itinerary. |
| Commercial partner or sponsor | A credible proposition with measurable value | Clear offers, labeled paid services, independent editorial selection and evidence for reach claims. |

The public festival tone should be approachable, lively and diverse. Optional private collector services need not create an exclusive public identity. Use global fairs as service and production references. Set local access policy and make no claim to their status.

## Economics to explain accurately

Core artist listing is free and Southwest Art takes **zero commission on direct-artist website sales**. Full-art-price payout requires funded processing. Do not promise an unfunded net amount. Gallery representation terms, artist payments, tax and shipping remain distinct.

The current founding paid-profile **proposal** is:

| Category | Target paid profiles | Proposed monthly price | Modeled gross/month |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Galleries | 12 | $150 | $1,800 |
| Restaurants | 8 | $99 | $792 |
| Lodging | 8 | $199 | $1,592 |
| **Total** | **28** | | **$4,184** |

The calculation assumes full uptake. The project has earned no revenue or profit from it. Artist profiles are not part of this paid cohort. Secure cash for staffing, service, processing, tax and other costs. Eligible completed experience referrals, services and sponsorship are other proposals under explicit agreements. [Commerce](../website-spec/05-COMMERCE-PRICING-AND-OPERATIONS.md) and [compensation scenarios](09-Founder-Compensation-and-Hiring-Scenarios.md) describe options. The founders have agreed no pay.

Regional representatives and a proposed 10% share of “sales” are an unresolved idea. Define the revenue base, payer, attribution, refunds and cost before agreeing terms. The project has appointed no representative. The project has no authority to charge an art-value commission. Test any future compensation structure against the artist promise and actual available operating revenue.

## Brand assignment to scope together

Start with the smallest coherent identity that makes the art, service and place credible. Develop further applications only when content, demand and funding warrant them.

| Stage | Possible outputs to agree | Readiness / review |
|---|---|---|
| Scope conversation | Desired role, actual available hours/start, fee/funding, constraints, decision-maker and review cadence | Conversation only. Not an unpaid design assignment. |
| Positioning and starter identity | Audience/promise, naming assessment, one agreed direction, essential type/color/logo/layout rules, image/credit principles | Small written scope, rights, fee, rounds and acceptance criteria agreed before production work. |
| Core applications | Mobile/desktop home, artwork/detail and partner presentation examples. Useful copy/voice guidance and reusable components | Review with representative buyers/artists and actual content. Prototype direction remains open to change. |
| Later physical expression | Artist campaign collaboration, signs, invitations, installations or merchandise | Separate artist licenses, budgets, production quotes, site/cultural authority and demand. No inventory order assumed. |

The working name **Southwest Art** needs clearance because an established art publication uses it in the same market. Retain it provisionally without treating naming as settled. See the [website specification](../website-spec/README.md) for the existing source and qualification.

Creative standards: let the art lead. Preserve true color, proportions and whole-work viewing. Use typography and spacing that make facts easy to read. Make paid profiles and unavailable items clear. Design focus/error/loading states. Allow discovery without using chat. The brand must work across restrained digital presentation and an inclusive physical event. Pay, credit and license local artist collaborations. Cultural motifs are not free decorative assets.

A first review should answer whether the prototype makes the mission and next action clear, feels credible at a serious art price, welcomes a first buyer, works on a phone, and gives enough context to ask an informed question. Identify the most consequential improvements before expanding the page count or commissioning merchandise.

## Growth and inspiration: separate hypotheses from commitments

Taos is the proof of concept. A Denver hub and representatives in Santa Fe/Phoenix/Denver are future hypotheses requiring demand, cost, local-leadership and capacity evidence. The project has no geographic-expansion approval.

Naoshima is a useful art/place/patient-backing reference. Its official history traces the initiative to Fukutake Publishing/Benesse and a municipal cultural/educational vision. It does not support an oil-company origin. Do not repeat an unverified “Japan’s most-visited island” ranking. The precedent suggests long-term backing and coordinated destination development. Local funding needs its own plan. [Official history, checked October 23, 2026](https://benesse-artsite.jp/en/about/history.html).

A reported Santa Fe event involving “43 international galleries” needs an event name, year and primary evidence before it becomes a market claim. An acquaintance, portfolio or reference website does not establish an available team member, sponsor or partner. Sponsorship/recruitment discussions need an approved proposition and actual permission before outreach.
