**December 18, 2026, 11:59pm Mountain:** New Mexico Arts final deadline, if eligible and authorized. Prepare materials early enough for the actual applicant's review. This is an external deadline inside days 31–60, not a website launch condition.

**Gate 2 — December 30:** review observed task success, rights-cleared inventory, buyer interest, staff effort and funding. Funded readiness, not the date, determines whether real selling or acquisition experiments can start.

## Days 61–90: December 31–January 29

- Grow toward ten willing artist/seller relationships and sixty verified works if appropriate; publish fewer if that is the honest cleared catalog.
- If authorized selling is ready, aim to learn from five fulfilled purchases and $25,000 completed art value. These are conditional learning thresholds, not required transactions before readiness or a forecast.
- Track qualified inquiries, useful viewings, quotes, delivery and artist settlement; report shortfalls and the actual denominators.
- Test a small HeyTaos or hospitality referral only if terms, content permission, capacity and funding are agreed. A partner pilot has its own dates; it is not assumed to start November 1.
- Reassess the 2027 sales case from actual inventory, buyer evidence and vendor quotes. Do not fund zero-commission website operations using the older illustration's artist fee assumptions.

**Gate 3 — January 29, 2027:** choose the next funded experiment or smaller scope. A five-purchase pilot does not prove a $3m business. An unfinished production review is a reason to keep improving the prototype, not bypass the review.

## Funding and event-readiness gates

Before new commitments, maintain a rolling thirteen-week cash forecast, match restrictions to expenses and protect artist, tax, refund and shipping liabilities. The scenario's art-sales targets and unawarded grants are not available money. Do not add website estimates to the earlier integrated illustration without eliminating overlap and rebuilding the budget.

By **July 15, 2027**, if proceeding toward a fall event, confirm non-cancellable costs can be paid, obtain cancellation terms and provide an adequate reserve. If capacity/funding is inadequate, reduce scope.

Thirty days before the **actual confirmed opening**, require venue/date authority, responsible safety/access reviews, insurance/seller agreements, verified inventory, rehearsed payments/settlement and contingency communication. No September date is invented by this roadmap. Extend closeout beyond October 31 where the actual edition requires it; do not misdate sales to fit year one.

## Operating rhythm

From kickoff, review demand, catalog gaps, service workload and cash weekly; reconcile authorized payments and delivery separately. Use the [weekly scorecard](../templates/Weekly-Scorecard.md), whose project year begins November 1. If founder availability is limited and no paid operator is funded, stay with a small content/design/concierge pilot. A full event requires a separate dedicated team and approved budget.
