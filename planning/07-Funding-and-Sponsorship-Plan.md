## Proposed sponsorship inventory

The following is a **test offer**, not a rate card backed by audited audience numbers. The base $125,000 mix is one $40,000 lead partner, three $15,000 partners, and eight $5,000 community supporters. Offer smaller contributions when useful; do not let a large sponsor control selection.

| Offer | Proposed ask | Useful benefits to negotiate | Protected boundaries |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Lead public-access partner | $40,000 | Credit for underwriting free access and artist support, agreed event acknowledgment, aggregate outcome report | No artistic veto, no undisclosed endorsement or buyer data. |
| Artist opportunity partner | $15,000 | Underwrite approved participation, teaching, handling, or access costs; documented outcomes | Funds do not purchase jury decisions or artist exclusivity. |
| Community supporter | $5,000 | Specific useful program support, factual acknowledgment, staff engagement where appropriate | No invented reach or guaranteed sales return. |
| Commercial service / advertising | Scope-dependent | Clearly labeled placement, training, content service, or co-produced licensed material | Separate agreement and appropriate commercial/tax accounting. |

IRS distinguishes simple acknowledgment from advertising and substantial benefits; accounting must classify each package rather than assuming every sponsorship payment is a charitable gift. [IRS guidance](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/advertising-or-qualified-sponsorship-payments).

## Prospecting and stewardship

Research potential hospitality, banking, professional-service, art-handling, design, real-estate, regional employer, and philanthropic partners. Prioritize existing trusted relationships. Taos Community Foundation and other local funders are leads to investigate, not presumed open programs or eligible awards. Tourism entities may support destination marketing subject to their current rules; do not invent co-op funding availability.

Maintain a private pipeline: entity, relationship owner, mission fit, actual open opportunity, eligibility evidence, ask, restrictions, decision date, probability, next step, and cash date. A weighted pipeline is a planning tool; neither it nor a verbal pledge is spendable cash.

Before an ask, prepare a one-page problem/offer, evidence and limitations, a scoped budget, an accountable entity, and specific benefits. After agreement, track deliverables and restricted expenditures. Report what was achieved, including missed targets, without disclosing private artist or buyer data.

## Grant writing discipline

Use AI for structure and editing only after humans establish the facts. Do not invent artist biographies, participation counts, historic sales, demographic reach, partnerships, letters of support, or commitments. Verify every budget line and current guideline. Have the authorized applicant approve the actual submission. Keep application files and supporting financial/legal documents outside the public repo unless explicitly cleared.
