## Commercial and technical boundaries

Build the teaser before pursuing funding. The discussions raised display ads, newsletter sponsorship, paid listing tools, paid itinerary use after a free session and a commission on some platform transactions. These are hypotheses. Test advertiser demand and the cost of delivering a useful itinerary before pricing them. Paid placement must be labeled and separated from editorial recommendations. Direct-artist website sales retain zero ArtSouthwest commission. A finance owner and defined settlement and reporting controls precede billing. Keep prior festival projections separate.

The project has no adopted name, board, equity offer, investor commitment or approved regional expansion beyond the two-city catalog. The long-term regional and staffing ideas belong in future planning. Choose a distinctive name based on fit and clearance. Google treats domain words as one of many relevance signals and limits extra credit for exact-match domains. [Google Search ranking guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ranking-systems-guide#exact-match-domain-system).

The meeting discussed a React build. The GitHub Pages reference prototype is buildless. Use the existing source and demo path for the first review. Evaluate a React migration as a technical decision when the builder can demonstrate an advantage for catalog or editorial work.
