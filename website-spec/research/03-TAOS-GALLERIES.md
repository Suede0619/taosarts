# Twelve Taos gallery recruitment targets

**Research snapshot: October 19, 2026. Gallery recruitment shortlist.** Count twelve distinct businesses or groups. The list establishes no paid partnership, price ranking or gallery endorsement. Do not count Parsons's two venues as two separate targets.

Collector credibility comes from the actual program, object information, artist relationships and service. Price alone is not a quality test. This shortlist combines contemporary art, established painting, Indigenous/estate expertise and material-led work. Curatorial review should confirm the balance and resolve conflicts before recruitment. Paid profiles purchase clearly defined services and carry a disclosure. They do not purchase our editorial judgment.

## Page content and commercial deliverable

Each eventual enhanced profile receives an original approved introduction, curatorial focus, real venue(s), representation/estate distinctions, current dated shows, six to twelve approved object highlights where appropriate, complete practical information, excellent licensed photographs, appointment routing, and a visible **Partner profile** label. Work highlights reuse canonical records. They are not separate duplicate stock.

Default gallery-page actions: explore available works, view current show, request an appointment, ask the concierge, and contact a person. Publish complete object information. Unknown prices remain inquiry-only. Do not copy an entire gallery archive, automatically ingest mailing lists or promise sales the service has not demonstrated.

Proposed founding service price is **$150/month per gallery business/group**, with scope and venue limits agreed. Quote extensive inventory administration and photography separately. No targets have agreed to pay. [Pricing and mission economics](../05-COMMERCE-PRICING-AND-OPERATIONS.md).

## Exclusions and corrections from research

Bryans Gallery's own domain did not yield a usable catalog and a [secondary listing](https://wanderlog.com/place/details/1714143/bryans-gallery) says permanently closed. Confirm the business directly before considering it. An old art-walk map cannot establish current operation. Familiar historic Hulse/Warman and Untitled domain references did not establish a current Taos business in this retrieval. One domain showed unrelated content. The other identified a different city. Neither is in the twelve. Do not follow or republish a recycled domain merely because its name appears in a historical Taos guide.

The twelve are a recruitment list. A smaller group with excellent approved records and dependable viewing service is preferable at launch to twelve unverified profiles that imply nonexistent relationships.
