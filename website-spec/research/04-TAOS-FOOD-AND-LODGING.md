# Eight restaurant and eight lodging recruitment targets

**Research snapshot: October 19, 2026. All sixteen are proposed targets, not paid partners or guaranteed reservations.** Selection reflects service, location, art/visitor fit and range; this is not an independently audited ranking of the region's most expensive businesses.

Paid profiles should help people make better decisions and produce useful referrals. They should not feel like an advertising directory. Keep the art first, give practical details promptly, and disclose commercial relationships. Fees and paid status cannot secretly determine concierge recommendations.

## What the paid service should deliver

Proposed founding fees: **restaurant $99/month; lodging $199/month**. Later standard tests are $149/$299 only after value is demonstrated. Bill after the contracted approved profile/service is live; offer a clear cancellation rule. Images/photography beyond a modest supplied-and-cleared set require a separate scope. No business has agreed to these rates. [Pricing and unit economics](../05-COMMERCE-PRICING-AND-OPERATIONS.md).

Each restaurant profile: a short original approved introduction; real cuisine/service period; accurate menu links and last check; two or three fact-based examples; price basis/fees; practical dietary/access information; correct location and permitted booking/contact action; three to six excellent licensed photos. Confirm current menus rather than republishing a complete copyrighted menu design.

Each lodging profile: original approved introduction; clear location; a compact room-type table; capacity, access and meaningful amenities; current booking route; honest price state; cancellation/fees from the actual quote; six to ten cleared room/property photos with exact room association. Room photos cannot be swapped among categories for visual convenience.

The concierge can answer and filter these structured facts, offer a shortlist and prepare a visit plan. It cannot confirm a table/room without a real provider response, guess allergy safety, invent an accessible feature, or promise an advertised brochure price. Referral income requires a separate approved contract/program; a UTM click or paid listing does not itself create booking commission.
