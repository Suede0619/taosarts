## What the paid service should deliver

Proposed founding fees: **restaurant $99/month. Lodging $199/month**. Test $149/$299 standard prices only after demonstrating customer value. Bill after the contracted approved profile/service is live. Offer a clear cancellation rule. Images/photography beyond a modest supplied-and-cleared set require a separate scope. No business has agreed to these rates. [Pricing and unit economics](../05-COMMERCE-PRICING-AND-OPERATIONS.md).

Each restaurant profile needs an approved original introduction, cuisine and service periods, checked menu links, two or three factual dish examples, price basis and fees. Include dietary and access information, location, permitted booking or contact links, and three to six licensed photos. Confirm current menu facts and obtain rights for any menu reproduction.

Each lodging profile needs an approved original introduction, location, room-type table, capacity, access, amenities and current booking route. Show the actual price status, quote-specific cancellation terms and fees, plus six to ten cleared images matched to the right rooms. Match room photographs to the actual category.

The concierge can answer and filter these structured facts, offer a shortlist and prepare a visit plan. It cannot confirm a table/room without a real provider response, guess allergy safety, invent an accessible feature, or promise an advertised brochure price. Referral income requires a separate approved contract/program. A UTM click or paid listing does not itself create booking commission.
