# Collector legitimacy, growth and launch service

**Proposed strategy, October 19, 2026. No outreach, partnerships, inventory commitments or sales have occurred.**

Project day one is **November 1, 2026**; year one ends October 31, 2027. Prototype work is authorized preparation; production and any seller activity retain their gates. [Common baseline](../PROJECT-BASELINE.md).

## Make a small collection worth a serious person's attention

Global reach begins with a credible, useful offer: a tightly edited selection, specific reasons to care, complete work information, excellent imagery, an informed human response, and reliable delivery. A distant collector needs confidence before buying a flight or a painting. Better service and truthful context matter more than a large directory or extravagant language.

An informed curatorial perspective should be supported by actual program knowledge and service capacity. Publish a concise curatorial introduction grounded in verifiable artist and exhibition evidence. HOUSE of FIRE is one potential viewing venue, subject to gallery agreement and independent selection; it does not automatically represent every listed artist or hold festival authority.

Build an initial body of twelve to twenty especially strong cleared works across several artists, then deepen toward the sixty-work target. Include both remote-purchase and private-viewing paths. Contextualize historical Taos without letting deceased artists or secondary-market inventory displace the livelihood mission. Publish accessible original prints alongside higher-value work, with accurate edition/reproduction labels.

## A complete collector service

Offer a twenty-minute collecting conversation, a concise five-work selection, detailed images/video, and a live remote viewing when the seller can provide it. Within the agreed staffed service window, acknowledge an inquiry within one business day; quote complex delivery only after the responsible operator confirms it. A knowledgeable response should answer the actual question, not forward an automated brochure.

An optional visit brief pairs selected work with confirmed artist/gallery appointments, relevant institutions, experiences and honest hospitality choices. A calendar invitation requires a confirmed appointment; an itinerary is not a reservation. Do not sell access to homes, sacred places or closed studios without consent.

For designers and advisors, support dimensions, materials, trade questions, client anonymity, installation requirements and repeat project briefs. Any trade/referral compensation is documented and paid from an agreed commercial source; the zero direct-artist commission promise cannot quietly fund it. International buyers get clear currency, delivery, duties and human support for their actual destination, with a quote where needed.

## Priority channels and experiments

These are proposed audience tests, not statements that named cities already produce our collectors. Start with reachable relationships in Santa Fe/Albuquerque and nearby destination markets; test a few larger markets such as Denver, Dallas, Los Angeles and New York before buying broad international awareness. Expand internationally when shipping/service demand and unit economics support it.

| Channel | Useful offer | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|
| Existing artist/gallery opt-in relationships | Personal introduction to a genuinely relevant edited selection | Consented inquiries, viewings and fulfilled orders; no transfer of private mailing lists. |
| Art advisors and interior designers | A professional sourcing brief and responsive object/installation information | Repeat briefs, qualified requests, completed art value and disclosed referral economics. |
| Museums, cultural groups and alumni networks | Relevant edited programming or an agreed visit, where the institution actually wishes to participate | Permission, audience fit, attendance and later qualified interest; no implied endorsement. |
| Hotel and experience concierges | Useful art viewing information, clear contacts and confirmed appointments | Referred qualified inquiries and completed outcomes, with any compensation disclosed. |
| Search and editorial | Artist-specific records, dated exhibitions and useful collecting/visit guidance | Relevant landing-page demand, saved work, inquiries and attributable sales rather than raw impressions. |
| Permission-based email | One excellent new work/story and a useful next step | Replies, viewings, purchases, opt-outs and repeat interest. |
| Carefully targeted paid media | A specific collection or confirmed visit offer | Cost per qualified inquiry and fulfilled order; capped experiments after the service works. |

HeyTaos is a potential complementary discovery/referral partner, not an agreed inventory or audience supplier. Propose links or event/feed collaboration only after deciding what each party receives and what content rights allow. The festival is a separate institution; coordinate dates and consent before describing the website as its official platform.

## Distinctive ideas to test

These are creative proposals, not claims of unprecedented invention or validated demand.

1. **A five-work brief before a flight.** Let a person describe interests, space and optional budget; return a short curator-reviewed selection and possible viewings. The visit becomes purposeful before travel is booked. Measure brief-to-viewing and viewing-to-purchase.
2. **One work, three distances.** Present a genuine whole-work view, intimate material/detail view, and accurate scale view. Add a short approved artist process account. Test whether this reduces basic questions and increases confident inquiries.
3. **A connected Taos day.** A saved work leads to a confirmed gallery/studio encounter, then a relevant public institution and a restorative experience. Explain the relationship editorially; do not invent cultural connections or require commercial bookings to see the art.
4. **A shared conversation around a shortlist.** A collector can invite an advisor or partner to a private selection without publishing contact data. Comments attach to actual works and dimensions, preserving consent and ownership of the brief.
5. **A visible artist benefit.** Report cleared, aggregate completed sales and artist payouts with median/distribution measures. A buyer can understand how the service supports working artists without viewing a performative donation badge or a false percentage.
6. **A response that remembers constraints.** The concierge carries the same selected region, budget, wall size, travel dates and saved works across sections, with visible controls to edit/delete them. Human staff receive only the context the visitor chooses to share.

Keep the experiments that measurably improve a decision or outcome. Avoid elaborate features whose maintenance would take time away from accurate stock, good imagery and prompt human follow-through.

## Pilot and success measures

Before broad launch, onboard a few willing sellers and partners, clear images, verify their records, and complete five end-to-end pilot purchases with genuine delivery and settlement. A research candidate is not an onboarded partner. Do not charge hospitality targets for an audience that exists only in a forecast.

Use a weekly funnel: relevant visitor → saved work → qualified inquiry → viewing → quote → verified payment → delivery → artist paid. Separate remote/local, direct artist/gallery, source channel and first/repeat buyer. Experience/hospitality success is measured separately, with completed eligible referrals and partner renewals. Observe human service time and processing subsidy per art order.

Illustrative target arithmetic, not a forecast: $3m completed art value requires 300 orders at a $10,000 average, 600 at $5,000, or a different verified mix. It also requires enough suitable inventory, actual buyers and fulfillment capacity. The broad festival model's assumptions are not a website conversion study. Reforecast from pilot data before committing acquisition spend.

## Owners and operational capacity

Proposed accountable roles: product/business lead; curatorial lead; editor/content operator; collector-service/fulfillment lead; and technical support. One person may cover several roles initially, but capacity must be explicit. Roles are proposed responsibilities, not named appointments or personal commitments.

Decide staffed hours, response coverage, seller confirmation process, exceptional-order escalation, content update capacity and a weekly operating budget before public launch. Paid profiles and referrals are useful funding, but the [economics module](05-COMMERCE-PRICING-AND-OPERATIONS.md) shows why they cannot alone guarantee a world-class service with fully subsidized artist processing fees.
