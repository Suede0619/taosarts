## What the later prototype should prove

A collector should recognize the quality and purpose, find relevant real work, understand its type and status, trust the people behind the site, and know the next step. A new buyer should feel equally welcome. A traveler should be able to make a plausible Taos plan without mistaking a draft itinerary for reservations.

Use one coherent visual direction first. Explore two representative compositions only if the reviewers want a comparison; do not build two full sites. Show desktop, tablet and mobile layouts with the same content and states.

| View / flow | Required demonstration |
|---|---|
| Taos gateway | Brand, region, art-first promise, editorial hierarchy and compelling licensed images. |
| Art discovery | Works/artists tabs; price, medium and size filters; palette aid; visible sort; no-results recovery. |
| Artist profile | Approved identity/context, clear works, direct versus represented route, process and contact. |
| Work detail | Whole-image presentation/zoom; scale and framed dimensions; edition distinction; price or honest inquiry; seller and delivery information. |
| Concierge | Contextual prompts; typed search; real-record results; apply/undo filters; sources; uncertainty; human handoff. A scripted simulation is visibly labeled. |
| Experiences | Ten-card curated list; filters; list/map toggle; detailed cost/duration/season/access and a correct booking route. |
| Gallery | Program, artists and a dated show; viewing request; clear Partner profile disclosure. |
| Dining and lodging | A dining profile with menu/payment facts, and a lodging profile with room choices and a date-dependent rates state. |
| Saved plan | Works and visits together; sequence with travel assumptions; sharing and inquiry consent. |
| Demonstration checkout | Clearly simulated order, total-cost breakdown, delivery choices, success/failure/refund-support states; no real financial action. |

Suggested prototype data depth: twelve to eighteen rights-cleared or expressly permitted work images covering painting, glass/sculpture, original print and reproduction; two or three fully designed venue details per section; all target profiles may appear as clearly designated research placeholders. The sixty research candidates are not automatically a licensed image pack. If permission is unavailable, use honest neutral placeholders or original commissioned assets rather than fabricating the named artist's work.

No real booking, payment, seller onboarding, public partner billing or external message is part of the visual prototype. Test-mode Stripe is a later optional implementation choice, not necessary to judge the design.

## Moderated tasks and success criteria

Recruit the proposed mix of collectors/advisors, first buyers, artists/gallery staff and travelers/residents. Obtain consent and avoid publishing personal participant material. Use a small formative sample to find problems; it does not establish statistically representative conversion rates.

| Task | What must be clear |
|---|---|
| Find an abstract original under $8,000 within a stated wall size | Correct constraints, dimension basis, price and actual available records; no silent substitution. |
| Compare an original painting, original woodblock print and reproduction | Users can explain the difference and the exact version priced. |
| Ask AI to filter, then undo | Visible controls and conversational state agree; undo restores the previous results. |
| Inspect an inquiry-only work | User knows availability/price need confirmation and cannot accidentally pay. |
| Request a remote viewing | Clear human recipient/service expectation and deliberate consent to send. |
| Plan a half-day from Arroyo Seco | Travel and opening assumptions are visible; closures and unconfirmed appointments are handled. |
| Choose a restaurant and room | Payment rules, sample-menu status and date-dependent room prices are understood. |
| Complete simulated purchase on a phone | Seller, all-in demo cost, artist policy and delivery are understandable; every action works by keyboard as well. |

Proposed acceptance: at least 80% of participants complete each core discovery task without moderator rescue; zero critical misunderstanding about a real purchase, seller, unavailable work, reproduction, paid recommendation or quoted total. Record errors, hesitation and trust concerns, then iterate. Performance and WCAG targets in the main spec must be measured on representative pages; beautiful screenshots alone do not pass.

## Review package and explicit decisions

The future prototype review should include a clickable private preview, mobile and desktop screens, component/token reference, content/rights status, observed usability findings, unresolved states, and a scoped production estimate. Reviewers approve specific outcomes and remaining changes, not an unseen future implementation.

Production authorization follows prototype acceptance and a funded operating model. Before live release, secure seller/partner permissions, image licenses, confirmed inventory, operator responsibilities, payment/fulfillment readiness, staff capacity and final accessibility/security/performance validation. Launch a small private buying pilot before broad promotion.

Suggested sequence after approval: one week to resolve the visual direction and content permissions; two to three weeks for the coherent prototype and formative revision; then scope production from the approved design and operational readiness. These are planning ranges, not promised delivery dates or a current authorization.
